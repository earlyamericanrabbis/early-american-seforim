This is where the text of the derasha goes. Write it, or paste it, as ordinary paragraphs, with a blank line between paragraphs.

A Hebrew paragraph lines up right-to-left on its own:

בראשית ברא אלקים את השמים ואת הארץ.

Quotations from Chazal can be put in *italics* by putting a star on each side, and a footnote looks like this.[^1]

[^1]: Bereishis 1:1.
