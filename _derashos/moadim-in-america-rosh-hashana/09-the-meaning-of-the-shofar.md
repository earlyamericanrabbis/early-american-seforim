ROSH-HASHONOH

Esteemed Congregation:

The average man or worker does his daily work mostly by routine, schematically, nay, mechanically, without much thinking, but habitually; the idea is lacking or worn out (to speak with W. James).

This is not so with intelligent people. They do almost everything with concentrated understanding. Their work is, therefore, more perfect and usually of a higher quality, very often leading also to new improvements and even to new inventions. The fact is ergo that the more often the intelligent person puts an idea into practice, the more does his understanding for the idea behind it develop.

Dear Friends, the Jewish people is considered as a "<span dir="rtl">עם חכם ונבון</span>"a people of wisdom and insight, as it is said: "*Keep therefore and do them; for this is your wisdom and your understanding in the sight of the nations, which shall hear all these statutes, and say: 'Surely this great nation is a wise and understanding people.'* "[^1] Let us therefore at this solemn occasion of the New Year, when every Jew is certainly thoughtful to fulfill the commandments of the day in perfect manner, endeavor to understand the meaning of the various sounds of the trumpet.

Our sages in the treatise of Rosh Hashonoh explain that a <span dir="rtl">תקיעה</span> is the sound of joy, <span dir="rtl">שברים</span> broken disconnected sounds, like sighs are sounds of lamentation and alarm. And so we read in the Holy Scripture: "*And on the day of your gladness and your appointed festivals and the beginning of your month shall ye blow a thekiah, with the trumpets*" etc.[^2] but "*If you go to war against the oppressor that oppressed you, then shall ye blow an alarm (a th'ruah) with the trumpets; and ye shall be remembered before the Lord your God, and ye shall be saved from your enemies.*"[^3]

In other words, the Shvarim and Th'ruah are to remind us to return to our God, with all our heart and soul; to decide earnestly and sincerely to make good all the wrongs which we have done in the past year. For only then can we expect to avert a severe verdict from above. In the Book of Zohar we read: "*Israel is privileged to obtain divine grace for themselves, to effect that our Heavenly Father leaves the bench of severity and replaces it with the chair of mercy and pardon; and all this through the blowing of the Shofar.*"[^4]

Yes, Friends, by blowing the Shofar with the proper understanding.

Let us then, my dear friends, be reminded by the sounds of Sh'varim and Th'ruah, as in a time of distress and war, to return to our God intelligently and conscientiously, so that we may be remembered before Him at this solemn Day of Remembrance "<span dir="rtl">יום הזכרון</span> this day of Trial;" <span dir="rtl">יום הדין</span> to the effect that He, our merciful Father in heaven, shall decree upon us a favorable decree and grant us a very happy and prosperous New Year, a year in which we shall hear the joyful sound of the Th'kiah, of the Geulah Sh'leymah.

Amen
