*"And Abraham returned to his young men," and Isaac—where is he? Rabbi Berekiah in the name of the Rabbis from there: He sent him to Shem to learn Torah.*

(Midrash Rabbah - Vayeira).

*One of the reasons for the blowing of the shofar with a ram's horn—that the Holy One, Blessed be He, shall remember for us the merit of the Binding. And also to remember the shofar of the giving of the Torah.*

There are many Jews who raise as a banner their Judaism in that they are borne in the ranks of Israel and do not flee from within the camp. They carry upon their shoulder all the suffering of the people of Israel, and all the many troubles and the various vicissitudes that pass over them, they also take part in them.

But to guard and to do all the words of this Torah, positive commandments and negative commandments, in this they do not want to bring their neck and they are from mentioning all these. Sufficient for them is only the name Israel alone and by the name Israel he shall be called. And behold the shofar, which is a rouser to repentance (see Rambam chapter 3 of Laws of Repentance), comes and slaps upon their faces and shows to them their error that they are erring.

And that same shofar which is to remember the act of the Binding, the greatness of self-sacrifice, it comes also to remember the shofar of the giving of the Torah, that is to say, that there is not enough in this that he gives over his soul for the people of Israel, and he is bound upon the altar to be slaughtered together with all Israel and suffers everything in silence. But the shofar reminds him of the day of the giving of the Torah and the receiving of the Torah, that is to say, that one must receive the Torah and guard its commandments and its charges, and Abraham and Isaac shall prove it. That after the giving over of their soul in the act of the Binding, that Isaac stretched out his neck, and Abraham took the knife to slaughter him, nonetheless after all these he sent him in the academy of Shem and Eber to learn Torah. And we need to take for a line and plummet the conduct of Abraham and Isaac. For it to be good for us all the days.

And in *Avinu Malkeinu* we say: "*Act for the sake of those killed for Your holy Name,*" this is the Binding; and so we say: "*Act for the sake of children of the house of the master,*" and this is the study of Torah.
