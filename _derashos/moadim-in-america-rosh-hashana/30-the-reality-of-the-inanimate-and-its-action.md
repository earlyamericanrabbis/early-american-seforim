*“The Holy One, Blessed be He, said, etc.: Say before Me on Rosh Hashanah Malchuyot, Zichronot, and Shofrot. Malchuyot, so that you shall crown Me over you; Zichronot, so that your remembrance shall ascend before Me for good. And with what? With a shofar.”*

(Rosh Hashanah 16a)

The idea hidden here in this wonderful saying—that everything depends on the shofar, and mere saying is not sufficient—is according to what is agreed upon in the world of science: that in the realization of any matter, one must give it an awakening for the action of interest, by presenting a specific and clear goal. For the concept of interest exists only in things or actions through their positive value, and through the recognition of the benefit that is in them.

As the matter which is explained in the commentators, regarding that which is brought in the Sifrei: (Bamidbar 10:10) "*And you shall blow with trumpets, etc., and they shall be for you for a remembrance before your God; I am the Lord your God.*" "*And you shall blow with trumpets*"—behold these are *Shofrot*; "*and they shall be for you for a remembrance*"—this is *Zichronot*; "*I am the Lord your God*"—this is *Malchuyot*. For seemingly it is difficult: why were they mentioned in the Torah not according to the order from what our Sages of blessed memory said?

Rather, it is that the theory alone cannot be sustained in the soul of man, except by performing it in deed and in action. And this is what the Holy Torah said—not according to the order of our Sages of blessed memory—only *Shofrot* first, etc. Meaning, that if the theory will not be awakened by a deed beforehand, it will only become empty and will never ascend to remembrance . . .

But "*and you shall blow with trumpets, etc., and they shall be for you for a remembrance,*" etc. Meaning, only through a deed and action of the theory, which is done through the reality of the inanimate in that same field, shall it be fixed in you for a remembrance . . .

And this is also the intention of what he said above: "*And with what? With a shofar.*" That is to say— Through the blowing of the shofar, the saying of the Malchuyot—so that you shall crown Me—will be realized, and the Zichronot—so that your remembrance shall ascend before Me for good.

For you have no sound that will awaken the heart of a man with holy tremors so much as the sound of the shofar! As it is written (Amos 3:6): "*Shall a shofar be blown in a city, and the people not tremble?*" . . . And also you have no king whom they crown without blasts . . .

In order to give an awakening for the action of interest, through the presentation of a specific and clear goal, regarding its positive value and the recognition of the benefit that is in it, like a king who is crowned with shouts of acclamation! . . . And understand this.[^1]
