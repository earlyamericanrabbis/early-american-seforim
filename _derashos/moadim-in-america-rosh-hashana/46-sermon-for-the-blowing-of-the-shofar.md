*"Cry with a throat, do not hold back, like a shofar raise your voice, and tell My nation their transgression, and the house of Yaakov their sin"* (Yeshayahu 58:1).

And the matter is because behold, three sounds emerge from the shofar: the straight sound of the *Tekiah*, the groaning-groan sound of the *Shevarim*, and the wailing-wail sound of the *Teruah* (see Rosh Hashanah 35b). And since the purpose of the shofar's sound is to arouse to repentance, as the Rambam wrote in chapter 3 of the Laws of Repentance: "*Even though the blowing of the shofar on Rosh Hashanah is a Biblical decree, there is an allusion in it, meaning: Awake, you sleepers, from your sleep, and you slumberers, arise from your slumber.*" Therefore, the sounds are not equal, for behold, the congregation is also not equal.

There are among them righteous individuals ho do not need a major awakening; rather, the straight sound of the *Tekuiah* is enough for them. "*Return to Me and I will return to you*" (Malachi 3:7)—this awakening is enough for the righteous to return to Hashem. And there are among them intermediate individuals whose level is lower than the righteous, but through the groaning-groan sound of the *Shevarim*, they return in repentance, and to them it is necessary to announce with a sound of weeping and sighing, "*Return, wayward children*" (Yirmiyahu 3:22); through this announcement they will see and perform repentance. And there are among them wicked individuals who do not return in repentance except through the sound of the wailings of the shepherds, a sound of weeping, groaning, and crying with a voice of a burning fire, and to them it is necessary to announce with the sound of a *Teruah*: "*Return from your evil ways, and why should you die, O House of Israel?*" (Yechezkel 33:11). Also in rebuke, not all faces are equal: for a wise and righteous man, a hint is enough for him, and a wicked man requires words as hard as sinews—and this is what the verse stated: "*God ascends with a Teruah*" (Tehillim 47:6)—this is against the wicked, ascending with the Attribute of Judgment. "*Hashem with the sound of a shofar*"—this is against the righteous. This is what the verse stated: "*Cry with a throat, do not hold back, like a shofar raise your voice.*" Just as the shofar has three types of sounds—the sound of *Tekuiah* for the righteous, the sound of *Shevarim*, which is a groaning groan for the intermediate ones, and the sound of *Teruah*, which is a wailing wail for the wicked—so you, the rebuker, with the shofar raise your voice, with wisdom to speak with a faint voice of answering to the righteous, and with a mighty voice of answering to the wicked who are sunken in their impurity. Just as the prophet said (Zechariah 11:7): "*And I took for myself two staffs; one I called Pleasantness, and one I called Destroyers.*" "*And tell My nation their transgression, and the house of Yaakov their sin*"—and see regarding this in the book *Mei Yehuda*.

And with this, I explained in my book *Succat Shalom*, part 2, in the name of the Torah genius, the Malbim, of blessed memory, who explains the verse in Tehillim 69:31-32: "*I will praise the name of God with a song, I will magnify Him with thanksgiving. And it shall please Hashem more than a horned bull...*" And the Gemara expounded upon this (Chullin 60a): "*Do not read mishor par, but rather mishofar.*" And the aforementioned genius explained that two things are not done on Rosh Hashanah because of the Attribute of Judgment. The first is that we do not say Hallel on Rosh Hashanah because judgment is extended, as their statement in Rosh Hashanah 32b: "*For what reason do we not say Hallel on Rosh Hashanah? Rabbi Abbahu said: Is it possible that the King sits upon the Throne of Judgment, and the books of the living and the books of the dead are open before Him, and Israel should say Hallel?*"

And the second is that we do not confess on Rosh Hashanah because of the erasure or incitement of the Attribute of Judgment. However, it is brought in the holy books that at the time of the blowing of the shofar, one should confess, and see, the judgment will be sweetened and the mercy will be aroused, and the shofar flips from the Attribute of Judgment to the Attribute of Mercy. And this is what is said: "*I will praise the name of God...*" in a language of wonderment? Meaning, the name *Elokim* denotes the Attribute of Judgment—can I say Hallel "*and magnify Him with thanksgiving*"? Can we say this, when behold this is the Day of Judgment, during which I cannot say song and cannot confess, except "*And it shall please Hashem*"—this is mercy, which was flipped from the Attribute of Judgment to mercy—"*mishor par*", this is the shofar. Therefore, my brothers and my friends, the time of the blowing of the shofar is a time of favor to awaken in repentance with all the heart and soul, regret over the past, and acceptance for the future. And Hashem, may He be blessed, in His abundant mercy will atone for us for all our sins and our transgressions, and will give us a year of life and peace, a year of complete redemption, Amen.
