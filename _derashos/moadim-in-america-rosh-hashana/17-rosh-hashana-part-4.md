*And it shall be on that day, it shall be blown with a great shofar, and the lost in the land of Assyria and the outcasts in the land of Egypt shall come and prostrate themselves to Hashem on the holy mountain in Jerusalem*

(In the Musaf prayer for Rosh Hashanah).

Happy is the people that knows the *teruah*, Hashem, in the light of Your face they shall walk.

We found in the Torah (Parshas Behaalotecha) that at a time of trouble, may God preserve us, such as war, against the adversary who oppresses, they would sound the *teruah*. "*And you shall sound the teruah with the trumpets and you shall be remembered before Hashem your God.*" And at a time of joy they would blow: "*And on the day of your joy and on your festivals and on your new moons, and you shall blow with the trumpets.*" And when we pay attention to all the various nations from the day God created human upon the earth and until this day, behold, a sound of *tekiah* they were able to sound, and not a sound of *teruah*, that is to say, they showed signs of life only on a day of joy, that means at a time when they sat upon their land quiet and secure, their king from them and their princes from them emerged, but not at a time of trouble, may God preserve us. At the time that they drove them from their land to go into exile. Behold, a sound of *teruah* they were not able to sound. They intermingled with the nations that came there, or they were made to vanish and were dissolved and melted away they went, and their name and their memory was erased. But not so the people of Israel, which is able to sound even a sound of *teruah*, that is to say, that even in its being in exile, surrounded on all sides by haters wanting to annihilate it, nonetheless it shows signs of life, and strives and fights for its rights, its standing, and its existence. And this is: "*Happy is the people that knows the teruah.*" And why do they know even how to sound the *teruah*? Because they walk in the light of the face of Hashem. And as the verse concludes: "*Hashem, in the light of Your face they shall walk.*"

And when we look with a penetrating eye, we see two periods in the life of the people of Israel. Period A, that many of the wealthy and the learned betrayed us. They left the ranks of Israel and joined with the neighbors who lived among them. The plague of assimilation struck its roots deep in the heart of the nation. They did not take part in our joy and did not participate in our sorrow; and this day is a period that the poor and the workers betray us, they joined—that is to say—the majority of them, the International, and deny the future of the people of Israel, and say: "We shall be like all the nations, the house of Israel."

And this is what the prophet announces to us: "*And it shall be on that day, it shall be blown with a great shofar,*" for the expression "*and it shall be*" is joy, and so the expression *tekiah* is an expression of joy—two joys. And that is that it includes the wealthy who were lost from us because of their wealth, and this is in the land of *Ashur*, and *Ashur* is an expression of "luck". And so those who were cast out from us because of their poverty and their lowliness, and this is "*and the outcasts in the land of Egypt,*" *Mitzrayim* is an expression of *metzar*. "Narrowness", behold these and these shall return to us and prostrate themselves to Hashem on the holy mountain in Jerusalem.
