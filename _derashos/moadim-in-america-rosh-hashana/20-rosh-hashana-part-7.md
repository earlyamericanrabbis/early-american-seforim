*Happy is the people that knows the teruah; Hashem, in the light of Your face they shall walk.*

And it is necessary to understand, is the happiness of the people placed in the *teruah*, until it said: "*Happy is the people that knows the teruah*"? And who is he that is not able to sound the *teruah*? And so it is necessary to understand why the verse does not say: "*Happy is the people that knows the tekiah,*" and behold the *tekiah* is the first of the sounds. And so at the end of *Shofrot* in the *Shemoneh Esreh*: "*For You hear the sound of the shofar and listen to the teruah and there is none like You; Blessed are You, Hashem, who hears the sound of the teruah of His people Israel with mercy.*" Also here is written *teruah*, *teruat*.

But hinted in this is a great matter, for the *teruah* is different from the *tekiah*, for the *tekiah* comes for joy: "*And on the day of your joy and on your festivals etc. and you shall blow with the trumpets etc.,*" but the *teruah* comes for war and a time of trouble: "*And when war comes in your land against the adversary who oppresses you and you shall sound the teruah with the trumpets,*" and so in Yirmiyahu 4: "*My soul hears the teruah of war.*"

And behold the people of Israel which is oppressed and crushed all day. Oppressed and afflicted, and war is upon it from all sides, and nonetheless it is alive and existing, it strives and hopes. And the people of Israel shows in this its strength and its might, that it is not only able to blow the *tekiah* at a time of joy and at a time when it is good for it and rest and quiet are all around, but it is able also to sound the *teruah*, that is to say, that it has a standing in life even at a time when it is narrow for it, and from rest they chased it. Behold, a people that has such powers, truly happy is it. And not all the nations are capable of this—of the *teruah*.

And this is the words of the Midrash, Emor 29, Rabbi Yoshiyah said: It is written, "*Happy is the people that knows the* *teruah*"; and do the nations of the world not know how to sound the *teruah*? How many horns they have, how many bugles they have, how many trumpets they have, and yet you said: "*Happy is the people that knows the teruah.*" Rather, that Israel know how to appease their Creator with the *teruah*, and He rises from the throne of judgment to the throne of mercy, and is filled with mercy for them and turns for them the attribute of judgment to the attribute of mercy.

And this is as we wrote, that for the people of Israel the attribute of judgment, which is *teruah*, turns to the attribute of mercy. And Israel remains alive and existing.

And the verse explains: "*Hashem, in the light of Your face they shall walk*". The power of the people of Israel is that it is able even to sound the *teruah*. Because in the light of the face of Hashem it surely walks, and the light of the face of Hashem is the Torah.

For "*in the light of Your face You gave to us a Torah of life and love of kindness*". And the Torah, it has two names: Torah of truth and Torah of life. And every place where there is truth, there is life.

And the Holy One, Blessed be He, who is alive and existing forever, behold His seal is truth. And only the Torah in its power is to bestow life to all those who meditate in it, and not the rest of the wisdoms, deeds of humans.

Because all the wisdoms that are in the world, we are not able to decide upon them that they are truth in their ultimate truth, and therefore even in this enlightened generation, the generation of Enlightenment, a generation that boasts that "*no secret is hidden from it*", and every hidden thing is revealed before it, behold there are many of the heads of the enlightened and learned who are evil and sinners, corrupters of character, who strive for human blood and to turn the dish upon its face \[overturn everything\], and therefore this enlightenment does not have in its power to bestow life to those who engage in it, and therefore a nation goes and a nation comes, but Israel stands forever.
