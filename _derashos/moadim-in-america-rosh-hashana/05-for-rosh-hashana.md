Beautiful is the Synagogue arrayed in festive vestments. Upon this day the ornaments, which greatly become the handmaid of the Hebrew religion, shine with brilliancy.

<span dir="rtl">מה טבו אהליך יעקב משכנתיך ישראל</span>

"*How goodly are thy tents O Jacob; thy tabernacles O Israel!*"

Yes: Beautiful are the Synagogues, where an assembly of worshippers throng. They beam forth the light of the Divine presence. Soul-stirring is the appearance of the Sanctuaries, when the adorers of the One God, crowd their courts. The sight inspires glowing thoughts.

And who but yourselves, dear Brethren! gives this shrine the comely form it now exhibits? With you in a devout attitude, it bears the looks of a Tabernacle resplendently glorious. Deprived of your attendance, it sinks into a homely tent. With you in vast multitudes, ideas soar aloft; Your continued absence dampens the most ardent of human spirits. Oh! Blessed be the day of memorial that draws you all toward our holy ark. Blessed the hours in which your minds gather strength to stand by the patriarchal faith to the last. You have come to renew your vow of fidelity to the King enthroned in the highest, to acknowledge Him the sole Creator with every pulsation of the national heart, the sole Creator and Judge of the universe. The distinct existence of our race will never be put out. The belief we cherish, will, at no time, merge into other creeds, so long as the spot is visited, where we teach one another to preserve our identity incorrupt.

Urgent reasons led the Sages to deprecate Hebrew men's dwelling in places destitute of a Synagogue. They knew the power it wields for good. They knew that the lack of a central point, around which members of the same lineage may rally, must loosen the bond of union. The callings we follow are naturally various. Our daily wishes and designs tend to in countless directions. The enlarging of one's sphere of actions occupies most of our attention; constant strivings to improve the condition of one's household, scarcely allow our distinguishing from other men, those who claim with us a Jewish descent. Here we meet as brethren. Here we feel that a common interest unites us indissolubly. The conventional separations observed in social circles are dropped in the house of the same loving Father. Our hearts jointly pant for a benign look of that Being we recognize as the arbiter of our destiny.

The Synagogue is a mighty rampart, against which the engines of our spiritual destruction spent their force in vain. A soothsayer of old, whom a king inimical to our fathers would fain have bribed to do them harm, pronounced a prophetic blessing instead of a grievous curse.

<span dir="rtl">מה טבו אהליך יעקב משכנתיך ישראל</span>

"*How goodly are thy tents, O Jacob! thy tabernacles O Israel*";

exclaimed the Divinity, speaking through his lips. Habitations of prayer and of learning will uphold this people's real greatness. So our teachers metaphorically interpret the utterance of the gentile Seer. And when asked why Balaam compared these institutions to streams spreading out far and wide <span dir="rtl">כנחלים נטיו</span> they answer as follows. In like manner as the body is Cleansed in running water, so is the soul purified in the sacred communion of the Lord, and in the unfolding of His word.

Dear hearers! That healthful result cannot be obtained by a single plunge, or by rarely bathing into the stream of salvation which flows from the sanctuary. You have read that Elisha prescribed for the leprous general of the Syrian army a sevenfold ablution in the Jordan. And that was to remove the spots clinging to the flesh. Will we not need wash seven times seven to efface the stains soiling the spirit? And again seven times seven lest pollution adhere unto us unawares, through a contact with the impurities floating in the world wherein we move?

To determine upon that all important resolve, you would be required to practice the self control displayed on this day of memorial. It would be necessary that every Sabbath you detach yourselves from pursuits that yield a temporary profit. Nay: trusting Him, on whose arm creation hangs for support, you should throng His house with national ardour. The exercise of piety, and the lending of a willing ear to the expounding of duty, would secure advantages, before which the gold of Ophir loses its value. We could learn to guide to guide our thoughts upwards, and meditate on objects ennobling our nature. But inestimable as that advantage is in itself it would be accompanied by other benefits which we cannot fail to appreciate. For in proportion with our obedience to the commands of God, will be the respect felt by children for their parents. And equal to the efforts we make to serve our Creator, will be the reward the King of Justice sets apart for the faithful. But See ye again a crowded gathering of the Congregations of the Lord in the Synagogues, and listen to the voice which cries "*How goodly are thy tents O Jacob, thy tabernacles O Israel*". The gentile admiring our religious principles utters that exclamation.

Fellow believers! At this hour of the Rosh Hashanah celebrated by the returned captives of Babylon, Ezra and Nehemiah observing a multitude touched by the word of the Law, so addressed them. "*Holy is this day to our Lord, grieve not yourselves, but let the joy of the Lord be your Strength"*. Thus also will I dismiss your assemblage. Fear not that Sin will debar your entrance into the presence of God. Lay now hold of righteous resolves and be of good cheer. They will unclasp for you the book of life. They will draw out the Seal of happiness with which a forgiving Father joyfully stamps the names of his penitent creatures.
