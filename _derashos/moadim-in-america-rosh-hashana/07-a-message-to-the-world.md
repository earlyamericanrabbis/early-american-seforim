ROSH HASHONOH EVE

Esteemed Congregation:

Beginning with the Sabbath preceding the ninth day in the month of Ab, we read our Haftorahs out of the book of Yeshayahu. We read from it the very stirring, pathetic, and severe sermon of "Chason", whereby Yeshayahu calls out in the name of the Lord that all the sacrifices, holidays, and prayers will be of no avail and will not prevent the approaching fatal destruction unless justice and righteousness are truthfully observed and accomplished.

Seven Sabbaths in succession have we heard his pleasant and gentle words of consolation, which shall give those of a broken heart healing and comfort; to the captive and chained, new hopes for liberty and freedom.

Today we are celebrating the commencement of the New Year, the day on which we give specific emphasis to the fact that the Eternal, our God, is the King of the whole universe, the sole ruler and guide of all men, their judge and avenger.

We are celebrating this day, however, at a time when our people suffer so much because of many injustices and much humiliation and oppression, all this after six and one- half million innocent men, women, and children of our people were exterminated in the most cruel manner in gas chambers and crematories. We are celebrating at a time when hundreds of thousands, nay, millions of Jews, robbed of all their possessions and belongings, uprooted and displaced, have now no country, no home, no place where they may go.

We are celebrating at a time when for these our misfortunate people the shores of their own land ISRAEL, their single hope and aspiration, their single place of refuge, the single place on earth where they are wanted, where they are welcome, are inaccessible, because: 1) In Russia and in all its satellites they cannot obtain permission to emigrate, even when not taking with themselves anything of their needful belongings, 2) because there and also in the Arabic countries they have not the financial means for the transmigration; and last and not least, because Israel is surrounded by cruel and tenacious Arabic enemies who persist in their unjustifiable claims, as known to every honest man in the world, and Israel has hence to cope with many complicated economic, political, and even military problems. We are celebrating at a time when all the 52 nations, the signatories of the mandate for Palestine, including our beloved America, who fully recognized that the Balfour Declaration was to help our people rebuild and reestablish our Homeland. I say, when all these 52 nations are not taking any serious steps to bring about a real and lasting peace for Israel, for all the Near East, and to eradicate and to make good, once and forever, all the injustices done to our people for nearly 2,000 years; at this time, when courage is imperative and new hopes peremptory and essential, on this day and occasion I cannot find, my friends, any better and more suitable words for a topic sentence than the words of this great prophet Yeshayahu, spoken in the second chapter, sentences 17-18: "*And the pride of (the) man shall be bowed down, and the arro- gance of the mortal shall be made low, and the Lord alone shall be exalted on that day. And the Idols (or brutal forces) He shall utterly abolish.*"[^1]

Esteemed Congregation: Before the far-seeing eyes of the prophet appeared everything which men consider great and mighty; all that they admire and gaze upon with wonder and perplexity, all that upon which they build their confidence and trust.

Here, he, Yeshayahu, saw the projecting cedars of the Lebanon in their majestic heights[^2], there the Oaks of Bashan in their defiant strength;[^3] here all the skyscraping mountains[^4], there every high tower and every fortified wall which were considered unconquerable,[^5] here the ships of Tarshish[^6], which furrowed the billows of the seas with irresistible might; and there the splendor of the precious spoils[^7], evidence of conquest and victory. All these impressive and forcible striking spectacles of human glory and strength form the vision of the divine revelation of the great prophet. Yet he saw also these cedars and oaks, the mighty armies destroyed, the vessels of the glorious fleet rent and torn apart, and all the trophies, the memorials of victory fade and become meaningless. He saw the Almighty God, in His infinite wisdom and righteousness engrave upon the tablets of history, for all generations to come, the sentence:

"*And the pride of (the) man shall be bowed down, and the arrogance of the mortals shall be made low, and the Eternal alone shall be exalted on that day. And the Idols (or brutal forces), He shall utterly abolish*."

My friends, all these words resound in a thousandfold strength out of the past and will resound always in the future. Let us therefore try to comprehend properly the meaning of the eminence and superiority of God, before whose might, wisdom and righteousness every glory, every vigor, and every magnificance must bend when He, the supreme King of the whole universe reveals Himself as the ruler and judge of all men.

Esteemed Congregation: Every morning and every evening Israel bows while saying the concluding prayer of "<span dir="rtl">ייעלינו לשבח</span>" and proclaims that the Eternal, our God, is the Supreme King of all Kings who expandeth the heavens and layeth down the foundation of the earth, and whose might is revealed through the whole work of the creation. On no other day are we, however, so deeply moved, so strongly affected by the idea that "*in truth He is our King and there is none beside Him*" as the day of New Year and particularly on this day 5707-1948 which brings to us, to our consciousness, human cruelty and injustice in such a staggering form as never before.

My dear friends: I see arising before me a phantom, the vision of a certain king: The (lion) head is shining gold, the arms glittering silver, the thighs stiff brass, the legs strong iron, but the feet, which are to support that massive body, are partly iron and partly clay. Yet one single mighty stroke, one mighty throw and destroyed lies pride and glory; all the gold, silver and metals are scattered towards the four corners of the world.

Yes, our history tells us not only once, what princely force has created, might and wealth for which millions of human hands had worked. However, when the historical day of avenge of the Lord came, all that was crumbled and rendered into dust. This brings to my mind the poem "Ozymandius" by Percy B. Shelley, which quotes on the only remaining ruin of a once mighty king's statue:

"*My name is Ozymandius, king of kings; Look on my works, ye Mighty, and despair! Nothing beside remains. Round the decay of that colossal wreck, boundless and bare The lone and level sands stretch far away.*"

All the mighty nations, such as Egypt, Assyria, Syria, Persia, Babylon, Italy, Greece, Spain, Portugal, etc. which have tried to destroy Israel are now only in the annals of ancient history or have become second or third-grade nations, spheres of influence, economic and strategic bases for others.

I wish that all the representatives of the nations who are now assembled in New York (and in Paris) to plan and formulate peace treaties, to stipulate mutual and correlative obligations; who are making an attempt for the realization of "*And they shall beat their swords into plow-shares and their spears into pruning knives;*"[^8] I wish that all these representatives of the peoples of the world, who are assembled to make efforts for the realization of "*no nation shall lift up any more sword against nation and they shall not learn any more war;*"[^9] I wish that they, particularly those of Russia, would read the Holy Scriptures and the Jewish history, specifically this second chapter of Yeshayahu, before it is too late.

Oh, it would surely be good if they were fully aware of the fact that the very same atomic bomb which by the grace of God, or to speak in the words of the Bible (once used by Mrs. E. Roosevelt) (to the effect) that the atomic bomb is the "finger of the Lord", which brought us victory and set an end to this second bloody world war; it would be good to think that this very bomb shall not turn out to be, God forbid, the element of destruction for the entire civilized world.

Dear Friends: We Jews pray every day, but specifically on the days of the New Year and the Day of Atonement, not only for the welfare of our nation but for that of all man- kind, but at the same time it shall never be forgotten that "*God seeks and protects the persecuted.*"[^10] "*Shall not the judge of all the earth do justice?*"[^11]

How good it would be if the walls of this house of worship could expand themselves and take in all men, particularly their leaders, to hear this honest message! Avenge, territorial expansion, seeking to create new spheres of influence, new maritime and land bases for economic or military purposes; injustice done to any people on earth, small or big, and use of might and force, at no time did and never will prevent war. This is a fact which has been proven since time immemorial.

My Friends: The Jewish New Year is also called the day of remembrance "<span dir="rtl">יייום הזכרון</span>, for before God Almighty comes the remembrance of every creature, "*each man's deeds and destiny, his works and ways, his thoughts and schemes, man's imaginings and achievements; for there is no forgetfulness before the throne of God's glory.*"[^12]

The strongest fulcrum of life is hope, particularly for us Jews. We always call out, in the moments when any other would despair and grow despondent, with the words of the prophet Yirmiyahu: "*For the Lord will not cast off for- ever. But though he causes grief, yet will He have compassion according to the multitude of His mercies.*"[^13] On this day of spiritual elevation, the Jewish people shall and will strengthen themselves with new courage, with new hopes, "*For God will not forsake us forever.*"

Let us hope then, my friends, that our merciful father in heaven will, for the sake of His holy name, for the sake of His people, and for the sake of all men, grant wisdom and insight to the ruling elements of the world, so that they may comprehend the fact that the ultimate result of "*The one who digs a pit for others, at the end he himself falls into the ditch which he made.*"[^14] Yes, very often, my friends, does one's mischief return upon his own head, and one's violent dealings come down upon his own pate.[^15]

I venture, therefore, to say to the rulers of the world, look out! that this friendliness towards one nation, at the expense of an injustice and disadvantage towards another shall not turn to be at some time even more disastrous than Dunkirk was.

Yes, rulers of the world, it is positively and exceedingly wrong to build anything for a prosperous future upon in- justice done to someone else.

I still do hope that the leaders of the world and all the people will recognize the great truth before it is too late; and that they will also acknowledge this fact in spite of all the tragic and regrettable current events, they still can have in the Jewish people an appreciative friend, without doing any injustice to the Arabs.

I venture also to call out, on this day of judgment of all men, to the representatives of the world, the makers and formulators of the peace treaties for the future, for God's sake, for the sake of justice, and for the sake of all men, I beseech you, to disregard the small oil advantages of the Orient, do not seek new military bases and spheres of in- fluence, for they will surely become a stumbling stone on the road of peace and mutual understanding between the nations; for all this will remain only a casus belli, which may bring about the destruction of the entire world. I also appeal to all the honest and conscientious clergymen, who are morally strong enough to make themselves free from the evil of prejudice; I implore you to use your influence from your pulpit and at every occasion in this so worthy and sacred cause so that justice, full justice, shall finally be done to the people of Israel, and that there shall finally be set an end to unjustified hatred and discrimination. I eagerly entreat all honest and good people in the world who have God in their heart and a sense of justice and loving-kindness, storm your Congress and Senate, or your parliaments, urge the United Nations, entreat your rulers; that justice shall be done to Israel which has suffered nearly 2,000 years under all kinds of injustice and discrimination; that peace and real good will shall permeate and prevail the world all over. May the godliness within men cause them to listen to the words of the prophet Yechezkel (33, 11) "*Return, return ye children of man, return from your evil way before it is too late.*"[^16]

Oh, my friends, I would be very happy if my pleas could be heard by the president of this, our beloved America, and also by all decent men of our country. As Americans, as people who love, live, and even fight and die for justice and democracy and humanitarianism, I beg of you, do not give your hand, your help, your consent to any unjust cause. America, all the nations of the world need your help if they want to rebuild their countries, their broken economies. Use your great influence at this so great moment for the salvation of the very unfortunate people of Israel.

In conclusion, my dear friends, let us rise and pray: Oh merciful Father in heaven, may it be Thy holy will, we beseech Thee, on this solemn day, when You decree upon the future of all men and upon the entire universe; have mercy upon all Thy creatures and also upon Thy people of Israel. Grant to the rulers and leaders of all the nations the real understanding for Thy holy way of justice, righteousness, and loving-kindness. Let them all cleave to Thy ways. Let them all find the road to real peace and mutual understanding. So that all of them, all of us, all men may unite into one single band to do Thy will with a perfect heart, with a perfect understanding[^17]; so that all men may deserve all Thy blessings, the blessing of real peace and contentment.

Amen
