"*Blow the shofar in the new moon, in the time appointed, on our solemn feast day. For this is a statute for Yisrael, a law of the God of Yaakov.*"

"*Shofar*"—improve your deeds. Chazal said in Rosh Hashanah (16a): "*The Holy One, Blessed be He, said: Recite before Me Sovereignty, Remembrances, and Shofar Blasts on Rosh Hashanah. Sovereignty, so that you shall crown Me over you; Remembrances, so that your remembrance shall come before Me for good. And with what? With the shofar.*"

The commandment of the day is with the shofar, as it is written: "*And in the seventh month, on the first day of the month, etc., it shall be a day of blowing the horn unto you.*" The matter of the shofar—by means of which our remembrance ascends before Him for good—is great and elevated above our human intellect, "*for it is a statute for Yisrael,*" a commandment without a reason.

(But) "*it is a law to Yaakov from his God*"—the explanation is like: "*and My laws you shall perform.*" (In Rosh Hashanah 26a, and there in the Mishnah): "*All shofars are valid except for that of a cow, because it is a horn*" (and the verse said "shofar," which implies a tube).

Ulla said: The reason of the Sages is because a prosecutor cannot become an advocate, in accordance with the dictum of Rav Chisda. For Rav Chisda said: For what reason does the Kohen Gadol not enter in garments of gold into the innermost sanctuary to perform the service? Because a prosecutor cannot become an advocate.

And the Gemara raises a difficulty: But behold, there are garments of gold on the outside for the service in the Outer Sanctuary? And the Gemara answers: We are speaking regarding the inside. The Gemara asks further: The shofar is also on the outside? And answers: Since it is for remembrance, it is considered as if it is inside.

Until here the Gemara. I heard this idea from our master, the Gaon, Reb Yisrael Salanter, ztz”l, and these are his words: It appears from this passage that at the time of the blowing of the shofar, each and every one of us enters inside and our remembrance ascends before Him, may He be blessed, as Chazal said: "*Since it is for remembrance, it is considered as if it is inside.*"

This is a great awakening for every man of Yisrael to stand now, at the time of the blowing of the shofar, in fear from the dread of judgment, and to examine his deeds. For at this moment he is remembered, and our remembrance will ascend into the innermost sanctuary, and he Heavenly Court scrutinizes his deeds. Just as the Kohen Gadol, at the hour when he entered into the innermost sanctuary, would stand in fear and dread, and would make a holiday for his loved ones upon his exiting in peace; we, how much more so! Therefore, we shall entreat His face, may He be blessed, that He incline our hearts to Him to walk in His paths.

The greatness of the matter of the commandment of shofar is a statute for us, as it is said, "*For it is a statute for Yisrael.*" Does He, may He be blessed, need the music of the blast of the shofar? He does not lack singers and musicians, as it is written: "*When the morning stars sang together, and all the sons of God shouted for joy.*" From the pleasantness of the sound of whose singing fell the army of Sancheriv as dead corpses before the wall of Yerushalayim, as Chazal said (Sanhedrin Chapter 11): "*He uncovered their ears, and they tasted the song from the mouth of the Chayos and died.*" Therefore, the commandment of blowing the shofar is a statute for us (without a reason), elevated above our intellects.

And so wrote the Rambam (in Hilchos Teshuvah Chapter 3): "*Even though the blowing of the shofar on Rosh Hashanah is a decree of the Scripture without any reason, there is a hint within it, as if to say: Awake, you sleepers, from your sleep, and you slumberers, arise from your slumber! Search your deeds, return in repentance, and remember your Creator—these who forget the truth in the vanities of time, and err all their year in vanity and emptiness which will neither benefit nor save. Look to your souls and improve your paths and your deeds; let each one of you leave his evil path and his thought which is not good.*"

The Remag also hints regarding the matter of the commandment of shofar ten matters, of which we will let the honored listeners hear a few, so that they may take them to their hearts:

1.  The shofar reminds us of the Standing at Mount Sinai, as it is said: "*And the voice of the shofar grew louder and louder,*" so that we should guard to perform the Torah which we received and that we should not turn away from it.

2.  The Binding of Yitzchak, that it should be remembered for good before Him, may He be blessed, in the merit of our father Yitzchak who stretched forth his neck upon the altar for the sake of the sanctity of His blessed Name; and to remind us that we, his children, should drawn after him to surrender our souls for the Sanctification of the Name like our holy father, whenever, God forbid, the time and the hour for this should come, and as our holy ancestors indeed did in the generation of the destruction in Spain and at the time of the Crusaders.

3.  It reminds us of the Great Day of Judgment, as it is said: "*For the great day of Hashem is near, it is near and hastens greatly, a day of shofar and alarm.*" See to place to the heart that a day will come when we will cast off the garment of physical matter and strip the body of dust from upon our souls; therefore, let us prepare provision for the distant path—a little balsam, a little honey—these are the Torah and good deeds to bring with us when we return to our Father in Heaven.

And behold, my brothers! Since now, at the time of the blowing of the shofar, it is an auspicious time, a time that our remembrance ascends before Him, may He be blessed, into the innermost sanctuary—now let every man resolve to leave his evil path and pour out his heart in his prayer, so that our remembrance shall ascend before Him for good, and to improve for us the writing in the new year which is coming upon us for good.

Let us leave all jealousy and hatred, dispute and quarrel; let each man love his brother to fulfill the positive commandment of "*and thou shalt love thy neighbor as thyself,*" and love the Torah to guard and to fulfill it. And then, our heart is secure and trusting in Hashem, may He be blessed, that He will bring forth our judgment to light. We shall be written and sealed for a good year, for a year of life and good sustenance, a year in which we will ascend with joy to our land, for a year of redemption for all Yisrael, Amen, Amen.
