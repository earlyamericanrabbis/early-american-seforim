*"Is Ephraim My precious son, or a child of delight? For whenever I speak of him, I surely remember him yet."*

(Yirmiyahu 31)

Exaltation and inspiration in my prophecy — the strength of my vision is vast through the single word "*further*", to reveal to us countless thoughts beyond research and infinite hidden treasures. For these words of the living God are on our lips, and the matters are elevated and pleasant... For the tiny infant from the time he emerges from his mother's womb is called "*son*", and he is considered a "*child*" when he is three or four years old; and when the baby begins to utter words from his mouth, then his father's love for him is boundless. (Yalkut Yirmiyahu 247).

And nicely did our Rabbis, of blessed memory, state: "*Rabbi Yudan said: When is a son beloved to his father? When he begins to speak. And so it says, 'Is Ephraim My precious son, or a child of delight?' What is 'a child of delight'? Like a three-year-old or four-year-old who begins to respond and they delight with his father*" (Midrash Tanchuma, Tetzaveh).

The children of the Jewish nation were called children unto Hashem, and our entire essence, actions, and prayers are that the Holy One, blessed be He, should sustain us like children. And so, there are various relationships in the life of a father to a son. There is times when the father's love is without any boundary, endless longings, and he coos to and kisses the body of his small son during conversation. And there are times when the relationship is completely different; the affection passes, and sometimes even turns to hatred, as was the case regarding Esav — as it is written, "*And Esav I hated.*" Love and hatred depend on the son's conduct, as the idiom goes: "*Your deeds will bring you near, and your deeds will distance you.*"

And behold, the seer's affection for his chosen nation is like a burning fire. He becomes impassioned when speaking about them. He compares them to a child at the stage when he begins to speak, as these are the means connecting him and his Father in Heaven. And before the eyes of the prophet is the Father sitting, and at His feet His small son, and He delights with him; his mouth spurts single words without any connection, and the Father listens and derives satisfaction from every sound of His son. And there are times when, with stammering lips, the small one expresses his request regarding toys, and the Father, with a smile, promises him to fulfill his desire with toys and sweets, and again an intense kiss — there is no measure to the affection.

So we request from our Father in Heaven, "*Understand what they speak,*" so that our prayer should be sweet to Him like the words of the small child to his father, who expresses his request in single words, and the father understands them and is happy to fulfill them. And the prophet calls out and advises: Take words with you! For through words, a soulful love will be bound between you and your Father in Heaven. And He, aside from His love to you during the output of your words, will remember you to satisfy your desire during times when your words are not heard, through His constant Divine love. "*For whenever I speak of him, I do strictly remember him further.*"

"For Hashem's portion is His nation, Yaakov is the lot of His inheritance" (Ha'azinu 32 \[corrected from 33\]).

I heard a fine matter from the mouth of my friend, the Rav, the Gaon, distinguished in supreme traits and elevated, the desirable man, the exalted person, Reb Chaim Tzvi HaLevi Rubinstein, shlit"a, from Chicago.

And behold, the Shlah, z"l \[of blessed memory\], said on the verse, "And Yaakov is the lot of His inheritance" — that the children of Yaakov are held by a rope \[<span dir="rtl">חבל</span>\] and connected in a constant bond with the Creator of the luminaries.

And it is forbidden for them to look from one end of the world to the other, for his lamp has gone out... "*Go, go, say to the Nazirite: Go around, go around, do not draw near to the vineyard*" (Shabbos 13a).... And forever hide yourself, do not enter, like the words of Shir HaKavod: "*I will imagine You, I will describe You, yet I have not known You." And these are also the meaning of the words of the wisest of all men: "For God is in Heaven and you are upon the earth, etc.*" (Koheles 5).
