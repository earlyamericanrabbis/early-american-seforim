*The Holy One, Blessed be He, is called today by many names. By the name God (Elohim), by the name King, by the name Father, "Our Father our King"*

*Avinu Malkeinu*, by the name Shepherd, and more and more.

By a king we found, although he is a king of kindness and truth, good and doing good, merciful and gracious. Nonetheless, according to the majority, after the verdict of the judged party, repentance does not avail him. And even if he regrets from the depth of the heart. Nonetheless, the king does not forgive him. But in contrast to this, it is in the hand of a king to do many favors for \[him\] whom the king desires his honor. And therefore there is an advantage and a disadvantage.

And by the father the matter is exactly the opposite. The father is not able to do favors for his son if the father is poor and his pocket is empty. But in contrast to this, the father is always forgiving and pardoning his son and never have we seen nor heard that the father would issue a harsh verdict against his son, even if he transgressed and did much evil. And if so, in the father also there is an advantage and a disadvantage.

And the Holy One, Blessed be He, who is called by these two names, by the name Father and King, it is because with Him there are the two advantages. He is called by the name King, because everything is in His hand, and He is Almighty. He is able to do good with each one and remembers for those who mention Him good memories. And by the name Father because He is always forgiving and pardoning, and even if one transgressed much and did the most difficult and the most heavy sins, nonetheless He forgives and pardons, "*for You are the Forgiver to Israel and the Pardoner to the tribes of Jeshurun, and besides You we have no King who forgives and pardons but You*".

And therefore the arranger of the prayers was precise and wrote *Salchan* /Forgiver and *Machlan*/Pardoner. It is like that which we say (Bava Metzia 33a), "*crouching*" and not "*a croucher,*" and Rashi explained: "*crouching*"—it is an occurrence for him. That he crouches under his burden at this time, and not "a croucher" who is accustomed to this. And if so, *Salchan* and *Machlan* indicate the constant forgiveness and pardon, (I heard from the enlightened young man Rabbi Chaim Bloch). And behold in a father we found another advantage that is not in a king. The son is able to confess before his father all the matters that which he is not able to tell before a king of flesh and blood. The son knows that the love of the father will prevail over all the iniquities that he did, and over all transgressions love shall cover.

And we confess and say: A human, his foundation is from dust and his end is to dust. With his soul he brings his bread. And by means of \[the fact\] that he needs to bring his bread, he is compelled many times to do things that are against his soul and his breath of life and against the Torah. But repentance, prayer, and charity remove the evil of the decree. And this is one of the reasons that we blow with a shofar, in order to rouse the people to repentance. And see in the Rambam (Laws of Repentance chapter 3).
