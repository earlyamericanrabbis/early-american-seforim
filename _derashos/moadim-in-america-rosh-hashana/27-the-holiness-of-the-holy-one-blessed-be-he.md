*“You are holy and Your name is awesome, and there is no God besides You, as it is written: "And the Lord of Hosts shall be exalted in judgment, and the holy God shall be sanctified in righteousness," etc. The Holy King.”*

(In the Shemoneh Esreh prayer of Rosh Hashanah)

The commentators wondered here: how is it possible to explain that He shall be uplifted by performing judgment? Behold, performing judgment is a descent for Him.

As is brought in the Holy Zohar: (Zohar Bereishis 108b) "*I shall descend now and I shall see,*" etc. And did the Holy One, Blessed be He, then not know, that He said "*I shall descend now and I shall see,*" when behold all is revealed before Him?

Rather, "*I shall descend now*" means from the level of mercy to the level of judgment, and that is a descent. If so, how did it say "*And the Lord of Hosts shall be exalted in judgment*"? And also, what is the matter that the Holy One, Blessed be He, is sanctified specifically through righteousness charity?

However, it can be said the idea in this is, according to what is explained in our holy literature, that this "*in judgment*" stated in the verse refers to this: that which men conduct themselves in judgment, and they are the observers of justice and judgment.

As the view of our Sages of blessed memory indicates also regarding this, in their wonderful saying: (Devarim Rabbah 8:6) "*The Holy One, Blessed be He, said to Israel: My children! By your lives, in the merit that you observe the judgment, I am uplifted. From where is this derived? As it is said: (Yeshayahu 5:16) 'And the Lord of Hosts shall be exalted in judgment,' etc.*"

That is to say, that the Holy One, Blessed be He, shall be uplifted and exalted only through justice and judgment—that which men do—for from where would they have the strength and the desire to be doing justice and judgment, if not from their Maker who shall be exalted?

As is explained in philosophical works, behold, anyone who conducts himself in justice and in judgment, and in uprightness, he is a sign and a wonder that he is a portion of God from above, because all that is in the whole is in the portion emanated from the whole.[^1]

However, the Holy One, Blessed be He, shall be uplifted more if the man performs righteousness to do good for his fellow, for in this he shows that there is the mediation of providence between the Holy One, Blessed be He, and men.[^2]

Therefore, it is that He is sanctified through righteousness, because by means of it His providence shall be revealed to man; shall He then not be sanctified? . . . and understand this.
