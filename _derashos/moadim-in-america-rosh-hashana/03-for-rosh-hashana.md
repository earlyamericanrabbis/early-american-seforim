**<span dir="rtl">רבונו של עולם</span>!**

Creator of the universe! At the thought of Thy essence, we stand appalled. When our minds presume to have, in their boldest flights, partly lifted the veil which conceals Thee from human comprehension, mists dense and impenetrable rise to baffle their searching. & no mortal has been vouchsafed the power of fathoming the secret of Thy existence. But to all the intelligent beings that people the earth, has been given the faculty of understanding Thy will and copying Thy deeds of loving-kindness.

Nature, which - upon this day we commemorate - issued from Thy plastic hands, bears the impress of Thy attributes, and teaches man wisdom.

Oh! that we pondered thereon! The smallest seed imbedded in the ground, and the immeasurable orbs that roll round the space, would alike point to Thee as to the provident Father, to the Beneficent Lord, to Him whose absolute will called forth the universe, that He might rejoice in the enjoyment of His intellectual creatures.

Then we all would, like brethren, dwell together in unity, feeling each others' woe, relieving each other's distress.

But the voice of nature, that speaks of celestial goodness, and of man's obligations, fails to reach us, because of the tumult which the passions raise.

By that are our thoughts diverted from Thee and our neighbors, and bent on the attaining of selfish ends. Yet, Thou O universal Parent, dost not withhold Thy favors from Thy children. Thou wilt still bid nature supply their wants and further their comforts, though they be unkind and ungrateful.

For, Divine love brought the world into existence, and with infinite love Thou sustainest its inhabitants.

But to recall man from the evil way he voluntarily chose, Thou didst send an infallible monitor. Israel first received counsel from that heavenly Guide, and, he imparted it to a vast portion of the human race… Thy word O God! which is perfect, enlightens the eyes of our understanding, It shows what is pleasing to Thee, because beneficial to ourselves and useful to our fellow-beings. Thy word O God! which is faithful, quiets the spirit, for, it proclaims Thee merciful and gracious, slow to anger and abundant in goodness and truth. To the erring it promises forgiveness, to the wilful sinners pardon, to the heedless the remission of their trespasses. Upon none who bow penitently at Thy footstool, upon none who lament the wrong they have done, wilt Thou, we are assured, pass a rigorous sentence.

On the day of judgment, Thou wilt spread out the mantle of Thy compassion, to hide the deformity of their souls. This consolatory knowledge derived from the volume of Thy revelation, has breathed into us fresh courage.

At the time that human actions are arraigned at Thy tribunal, we lay our hearts bare before Thy august presence. Mould them anew O Lord! Let every pulsation be henceforth a holy thought, every throb a noble aspiration. Do not cast us away by reason of the stains with which they are covered. But let the memorial of the primitive love which brought forth creation, lean Thee mercifully towards Thy frail creatures.

Blot out their iniquities for Thy name's sake, and grant their wishes. We pray for life, O ever-living God! that we may amend what is defective in our demeanour. We pray for health, that we may be enable to work with alacrity, for our eternal redemption.

We pray for prosperity, that free from pressing wants — we may devote our energies to the spiritual improvement of our children, to the elevation of Israel's character, and to the glory of Thy Divinity, O supreme Judge, Creator of the world, Support and Saviour of all mankind. Amen.
