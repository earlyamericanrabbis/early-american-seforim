*"And may our petition be sweet before You as a burnt offering and as a sacrifice."*

(In the prayer of the Priestly Blessing platform)

Many find difficulty here with the doubling of the language, for an *Olah* is also a *Korban*. If so, what is the reason that its matter was doubled?

However, it can be said that the idea in this is according to what is brought in the *Midrash Rabbah*: (Vayikra Rabbah 10:4)

"*Noah offered a sacrifice, and it was sweet to the Holy One, Blessed be He, as it is said: (Bereishis 8:21) 'And the Lord smelled the pleasing aroma.' Israel offered and it was sweet to the Holy One, Blessed be He, and we do not know which of them is sweeter to Him? Rather, in that He commands Israel and says to them: 'My pleasing aroma you shall observe to offer to Me,' we know that that of Israel is sweeter. This is what is written: 'And the meal-offering of Judah and Jerusalem shall be sweet to the Lord as in the days of old and as in ancient years'—'as in the days of old,' as in the days of Moses; 'and as in ancient years,' as in the years of Solomon, etc.*" See there.

For seemingly it can be said: how is it implied from this verse that that of Israel is sweeter?

However, it can be said according to what is explained in the words of our Sages of blessed memory: (Zevachim 115a) that this sacrifice which Noah offered had the law of a *Bamah*, for it was not permitted to offer on a *Bamah* except *Olot*, and as it is written regarding Noah: (Bereishis 8:20) "*And he offered burnt offerings on the altar.*"

As the view of our Sages of blessed memory also teaches regarding this, in their wondrous saying: (Menachot 73b)

"*Peace offerings of the idolaters are burnt offerings. If you wish, say it is a verse; and if you wish, say it is reasoning. If you wish, say reasoning: an idolater—his heart is toward Heaven. And if you wish, say a verse: (Vayikra 22:18) 'Which they shall offer to the Lord for a burnt offering,' whatever they offer shall be a burnt offering.*"

The commentators explained: because an idolater does not know the nature of a sacrifice, and when he vowed in his heart toward Heaven, he vowed that his sacrifices should be entirely to Heaven, which is a burnt offering![^1]

And behold, according to the instruction of the Mishnah there: (Menachot 113a), there is no “*pleasing aroma*” on a Bamah, and this is learned from a verse, see there. Therefore, it must be said that the sweetness of "*pleasing aroma*" this matter in Noah, is not like the pleasing aroma of the Tabernacle and the Temple.

Because in the Tabernacle and the Temple the offering was by way of command, therefore it needed to be with desirable intent; for this reason, there is in this an element of a pleasing aroma. Not so in a *Bamah*, which is only permission and not by way of command, and the entire reason for this is so that they should not be drawn to offer to idolatry; therefore, there is not in this an element of a pleasing aroma, since there is not in this offering a desirable intent.

Therefore, it is that which is implied from this verse, that the sacrifice of Israel is most sweet, because their sacrifice was according to command... not so regarding Noah... for the matter was only as an exception to the rule of other *Bamot*, in which there is no pleasing aroma at all...

Now the matter will turn correctly upon its hinge, regarding what he said: "*As a burnt offering and as a sacrifice,*" meaning like that burnt offering and sacrifice that Noah offered, which was sweet to the Lord, and the Holy One, Blessed be He, was exceedingly appeased by him—which this sweetness was not said regarding other sacrifices during the time of the permission of *Bamot*, for there is no pleasing aroma in a *Bamah*, but in his case, the matter was as an exception to the rule...

Likewise, we also request that this our petition, in the time when there are no sacrifices, shall be sweet before the Holy One, Blessed be He, as a burnt offering and sacrifice that Noah offered; just as the Holy One, Blessed be He, showed greater appeasement than from other sacrifices of the permission of *Bamot*, so may the Holy One, Blessed be He, also show us greater appeasement... and understand this.
