*"With this shall Aaron come": This is what is written (Tehillim 27:1) "Of David: The Lord is my light and my salvation; whom shall I fear?" The Rabbis interpret the verse regarding Rosh Hashanah and Yom Kippur. "My light": on Rosh Hashanah. "And my salvation": on Yom Kippur. "Whom shall I fear"—"the Lord is my strength and song," "when evil-doers come near against me": these are the princes of the nations of the world. "To eat my flesh": because the princes of the nations of the world come and accuse Israel before the Holy One, Blessed be He, and say before Him: Master of the Universe! These are idolaters and these are idolaters, these are revealers of forbidden relations and these are revealers of forbidden relations, these are spillers of blood and these are spillers of blood, for what reason do these descend to Gehenna, and these do not descend to Gehenna? "My adversaries and my enemies to me": in the days of the sun there are 365 days; HaSatan in Gematria is 364, for all the days of the year the Satan accuses, and on Yom Kippur he does not accuse. Israel said before the Holy One, Blessed be He: If a camp of Samael shall encamp against me, my heart shall not fear: for You promised me "With this shall Aaron come into the Holy," etc.*

(Vayikra Rabbah 21:4)

When we reflect upon this Midrashic saying, the question will be aroused: what is... ..the matter that "Rosh Hashanah" is mentioned here alongside the coming of Aaron to the Sanctuary? (which was specifically on Yom Kippur, as Rashi explained in Vayikra 16:3). Granted "Yom Kippur," it is well that the Rabbis interpreted it, to hint that on the Day of Kippur, when Aaron entered the Sanctuary to atone for Israel, he was not afraid that the Satan would accuse, but Rosh Hashanah—why?

However, the idea in this can be said, according to what is explained in the books, that the intention of the Rabbis who interpreted the verse also regarding Rosh Hashanah is to hint that, like on the Day of Kippur, the merit of the day itself causes for them, for Israel, that their sin shall be atoned.

As is brought in the Holy Zohar (Vayikra 62): This is what is written: (Malachi 1:2) "*I have loved you, says the Lord,*" and it is written (Devarim 7:8): "*out of the Lord's love for you,*" etc. And what is the extra love that He loved them? He gave them one day in the year to justify them, and to justify them from all their sins, as it is written (Vayikra 16:30) "*For on this day He shall atone for you, to purify you from all your sins, before the Lord you shall be purified.*"

And as the view of our Sages of blessed memory indicates also regarding this, in their wonderful saying: (Yoma 85b) "*Rabbi Akiva said: Fortunate are you, Israel! Before whom are you purified? Who purifies you? Your Father in Heaven; as it is said (Yechezkel 36:25) 'And I will sprinkle pure water upon you and you shall be pure.' And it says: (Yirmiyahu 17:13) 'The Hope of Israel'; just as a mikveh purifies the impure, so the Holy One, Blessed be He, purifies Israel.*"

For the intention is according to what the commentators say, (see also in Yismach Moshe, Vayikra 16:30). For any precious thing when it is corrupted, not every man, or craftsman, is able to repair it. For however much he might repair it, it remains in its corruption, except for a great craftsman who is specialized in making things like it—he is able to repair it. So too the soul of man, which is precious of the precious, which is a portion of God from above! When it is corrupted by the stains of the physical, what craftsman is found who can repair, or clean, such a precious thing? Shall all the physicians of Gilead suffice? For however much they repair it, and clean it, and heal it, it will not be repaired; therefore there is no choice but that the Craftsman Himself shall repair it.

And this is what they said: "*Fortunate are you, Israel, before whom are you purified,*" etc. Meaning, that the purification of your soul depends only on the Holy One, Blessed be He; therefore He... ...purifies it, because He is its Fashioner and Creator, shall He then not repair it when it has been corrupted? And especially since there is no craftsman besides Him.

And this is also the intention of what the verse said: (Vayikra 16:30) "*For on this day He shall atone for you, to purify you,*" etc. That is to say, because of this He shall atone for you, for "*before the Lord you shall be purified*": meaning, this thing is from the impossible, that a man by himself should be able to purify his soul, for is such a craftsman then found? Therefore, it is necessary that it shall come before the Lord, for He fashioned it and created it, and what craftsman will not repair that which his hands fashioned?

Likewise also on Rosh Hashanah, the merit of this day, from the love of the Holy One, Blessed be He, for His people, causes the mouth of the Satan and the accuser to be closed, so that He shall bring out a righteous judgment, after all the inhabitants of the world pass before Him like Bnei Maron, "*and all of them are scanned in a single scan,*" that is to say, that He shall look upon the deeds of Israel in general[^1], is it then not the merit of the day? And understand this.[^2]

[^1]: See in the essay "The Scanning of the Creatures and Their Deeds"
[^2]: See also in my book "Nechmad VeNa'im" Part 3, The Love of the Holy One, Blessed be He, for Israel
