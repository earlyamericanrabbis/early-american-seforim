*"And by the hand of Your servants the prophets it is written, saying: (Yirmiyahu 2:2) 'Go and cry in the ears of Jerusalem, saying: Thus says the Lord, I remember for you the kindness of your youth, the love of your nuptials, your walking after Me in the wilderness in a land not sown.'"*

(In the Musaf prayer of Rosh Hashanah)

Here the idea can be dressed—why the verse said "*after*" specifically. It is according to what is explained in the commentators[^1] regarding what we say in the liturgy: "*And remove the Satan from before us and from behind us.*"

For the way of the world is:

- *The Invitation***:** When they call a man to a feast, then the messenger sent for him from the feast walks before the man who was called, for he is certain that the guest will walk after him to fulfill his will . . .

- *The Arrest***:** However, when they take a man to prison, then the messenger who shall imprison him walks from behind him, so that he shall not flee . . .

And this is what he said: "*And remove the Satan,*" etc. For he, the Satan, he is the Evil Inclination, he is the Angel of Death.

1.  In the beginning, he entices \[the man\] to transgression to fulfill his desire, and the man—whose eyes are prevented from seeing—runs after him, and the Satan is before him . . .

2.  And afterward, when he leads him to judgment, the Satan walks from behind him . . . [^2]

Likewise also here, \[regarding\] what he said: "*Thus says the Lord, I remember for you the kindness of your youth . . . your walking after Me in the wilderness,*" etc. Meaning, that the Blessed Name remembers the deeds of the patriarchs who did everything from their will . . .

And the proof is that they walked after the Holy One, Blessed be He, in the wilderness, and the Holy One, Blessed be He, was before them leading them.

And this is the sign that they performed the will of the Omnipresent from their good will . . . and understand this.

[^1]: See also in *Yeshuot Yaakov*, Orach Chaim, end of the Laws of Tzitzit
[^2]: See also in my book "Or HaAggadah" Part 2, The Instruction of Perfection for Man
