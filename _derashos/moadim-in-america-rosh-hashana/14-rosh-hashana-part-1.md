*Rabbi Abbahu said: Why does one blow with a ram's horn? The Holy One, Blessed be He, said: Blast before Me with a ram's horn so that I shall remember for you the binding of Isaac son of Abraham, and I account it upon you as if you bound yourselves before Me.*

(Rosh Hashanah 16)

And just as we see in the kingdom of the earth, if some national hero put his soul in his palm, and placed his life in danger, in order to protect his motherland, and did and succeeded, behold this merit is remembered for good for his ancestors after him, until the end of all generations, so is the matter in the kingdom of heaven. Abraham our father gave over his soul for God in the act of the Binding. He went to slaughter his only son because he was commanded by Hashem, and the self-sacrifice is in two parts: first, to slaughter his only son whom he loved very much, and, second, the father himself will take the knife to pass it over the neck of his son. Hah! How awesome and terrible is this deed and how tragic it is, a merciful and good father slaughters with his hand his only son. And even more difficult and fearsome is the act of the Binding for Abraham our father. Abraham our father was the leader and the first who spread the idea of the unity of God and that the Holy One, Blessed be He, loathes human sacrifices, and only kindness and justice are the foundation of His throne, and truth and righteousness shall precede His face, and Hashem is the source of the good and the light And because of this he was cast by Nimrod "*in Ur Kasdim*" to go up on the pyre, and only by way of miracle was he saved, and so he was imprisoned in the jail ten years (Bava Batra 91), because he preached in the name of Hashem that idol worship is vanity, and finally he himself goes to slaughter his son, and in this he destroys until the foundation his good lesson that he preached to humanity.

And in truth the Satan came to him with this claim and did not let him fulfill his plot (Sanhedrin 89). And nevertheless Abraham our father did not listen to him; is there for you a self-sacrifice greater than this? And the merit of the Binding we mention today on the Day of Judgment on Rosh Hashanah.

And we say in Midrash Rabbah, Parshas Vayeira:

*Rabbi Hanina said: Why is the knife called* <span dir="rtl">מאכלת</span>*, because it renders the food fit to be eaten, and the Rabbis say: everything that Israel eats in this world, they do not eat but in the merit of that same Ma'akhelet.*

And we have more to learn from the Binding:

*Behold Isaac asks: "And where is the lamb for the burnt offering?" And Abraham said: "God will see for Himself the lamb for the burnt offering, my son"; and they both went together.*

We see in this generation, the great separation between the elders and the youths. The youths do not want to walk in that same path that the elders walk in. To the elders, Torah and commandments are dear and holy, but the youths—woe and alas! And they look rebelliously at the elders, and sometimes even throw dust upon the elders.

But you, the youths, look now at the act of the Binding and learn from it how Isaac the son listened to the voice of his father, and even though the matter was at the cost of his life; and it is a *kal v’chomer* argument: if he listened to the voice of his father to slaughter him, behold certainly every utterance of his father's mouth was holy to him, and he listened and obeyed all that he would command him. And this is: "*and they both went together*"; the son and the father went arm-in-arm with one another, and in that same path that the father walked, the son also walked.

And also for this matter we mention the act of the Binding, that our sons shall learn to walk in the paths of their fathers for it to be good for them all the days.
