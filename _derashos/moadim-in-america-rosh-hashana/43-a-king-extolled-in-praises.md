The Men of the Great Assembly established the formula of this blessing, for it is known, the statement of our Sages, of blessed memory: A person should not recount the goodness of his fellow, for out of his praise he comes to his disgrace, since there is no man on earth without any flaw or deficiency. And when people converse about a respected man, prominent among his people, and recount his virtues, they come willingly (or at first under duress and afterward willingly) to recount also a trace of a matter found in him, and this trace will impart a defect to all the virtues of the man.

However, the Supreme King, He is at the ultimate state of perfection, for there is no deficiency or excess in Him, and through praises it is impossible to come to any disgrace. Therefore, they established in the formula of the blessing: "*A King extolled in praises.*"
