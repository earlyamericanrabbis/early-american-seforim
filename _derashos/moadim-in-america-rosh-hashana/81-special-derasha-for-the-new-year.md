*What I said in a great congregation in my city in the year 5648 (1887/1888)*

*Rabbi Kruspedai said, Rabbi Yochanan said:* "*Three books are opened on New Year: one of completely wicked, and one of completely righteous, and one of intermediates. Completely righteous are written and sealed immediately for good life. Wicked are written and sealed immediately for death. And intermediates are suspended and standing from New Year until the Day of Atonement. They merited, they are written for life; they did not merit, they are written for death.*"

(in Tractate Rosh Hashanah page 16b)

And behold it is known what is difficult on this. Behold it is seen in the senses that the thing is not so, for most times we are seen to the opposite, that righteous, Heaven forbid, after the Day of Atonement die, and so wicked lengthen in their wickedness. Rather, that Tosafos already answered there that it refers to the life of the world to come, that there is the main life. And therefore even if it was decreed upon the righteous that he should die, it is for the purpose of his being in the world to come, and so to the opposite. And behold, according to these our words that the main life is the spiritual life, and also according to what the Vilna Gaon wrote that on the Day of Atonement the Holy One, blessed be He Himself judges the man, we are able to depict our daily situation in this example and in a pleasant parable.

In a certain country there was a king of lovingkindness, and all the sons of his country loved him, and served him in faithfulness. Once, upon the coming of the day of his sitting upon the throne of his kingdom (coronation - *kranatsye*), and his heart was good, he said to all the gathered ones: "Whoever has a request to the king, let him come and request." And men of valor approached and fell before him and before his feet and said: "Behold, you are a king of lovingkindness, for this we have loved you, and we are ready to go for you and for your kingdom in fire and in water. But concerning one thing we have to complain. And it is, behold at the time of the war, the custom is at the time of the war, when the fighting sides will request from each other for a day or two days to make a cessation and to rest a little. And then they gather all the killed from the field of slaughter and bury them. And then among those lying on the ground there are three categories there.

*Category 1*: they are the men whose body is strong as before, only that they were wounded with light wounds, and if they will lie in the hospital a few days they will return to their strength.

*Category 2*: they are the men of valor who already have no breath of life in them, and it is required to bury them in a Jewish grave.

*Category 3*: they are the men of valor who were struck and wounded with hard wounds, and they are endangered, but there is still the breath of life in them, and there is hope that if they removed the arrows from their body and will bandage their wounds that they will be healed.

And behold, the custom by you is that you send an expert doctor to visit the sick and all the men of the host who fell in the war, which to the grave and which to lay them in the hospital. And we will recount to you a deed now, what happened in the war, that there was a certain man of the army wounded hard until he did not have the strength to speak. And it came to pass when the doctor drew near to him and saw his weakness that he is not able to speak, immediately he commanded to bury him among the killed. This sick person understood the words of the doctor and began to hint with his fingers to show to the doctor that there is still vitality in him. And it did not help at all with his hints, and afterwards he girded the remnant of his strength and let escape from his mouth only these words: 'Am I not still alive?' And the doctor answered: 'Your life is not life, for your moments are counted, and you will also have torments, therefore your death is better than your life.' And so they did, that they buried him alive in the grave. Upon such cruelty our hearts will not be quiet and we are not able to forget. Therefore we request from you, my lord the king, that you yourself will go to visit the wounded, and we will be confident in your lovingkindness that sick persons like these you will command to receive them in the royal hospital, and they will heal them with precious drugs like the hand of the king, and a healing near to come." The king promised the men of valor to do so, and then the entire camp of the men of the army immediately knelt upon their knees and gave thanks to the king for this with a strong feeling until tears flowed from their eyes when they heard this from the king.

The moral is thus. Behold, according to what Tosafos wrote (in Tractate Rosh Hashanah page 16b) that it refers to the life of the world to come, that there is the main life. Therefore it will emerge for us according to their words that the life of this world is only a parable to the true life that is in the world to come, that the world to come is main.

And the moral is so.

The death of this world is only a parable, and the death of this world is not death at all. Except he who dies an eternal death, meaning to say also in the world to come, this is called death. And so the good and the bad of this world is also only a parable to the true war. And so it is in all the wars that are in this world, it is only a parable to the true war, which is the war of the inclination. And it is a great war, because he who falls in this war, his death is an eternal death. And also this enemy who shoots arrows at us all the year and seeks to destroy us from the world. And as it is written in Tehillim 37:32: "*The wicked watches the righteous, and seeks to slay him.*" And behold, the wounded in this war are also divided into 3 categories.

*Category 1*: they are the righteous, for even though they also are wounded with light wounds, for "*there is not a righteous man upon earth*" (Koheles 7:20 etc. And nevertheless \[<span dir="rtl">ומכל מקום</span>\] there is no danger in them, and with thoughts of repentance they will be healed and will remain in eternal life.

*Category 2*: they are the completely wicked, who also in their lives are called dead, as our Sages of blessed memory said (in Tractate Berachos 18b).

*Category 3*: they are the intermediates, who are endangered sick persons, for the Evil Inclination shot arrows at them in the limbs that the soul depends upon them, meaning that he tripped them in severe transgressions.

And nevertheless there is in them still the breath of life, because they believe in God and in His Torah, and are full of regrets on this that they sinned. And sick persons like these, if their visitor was of flesh and blood, he would certainly judge them to the dying without any hope for their healing. But since we wrote above in the name of the famous Gaon, the Vilna Gaon, that the Holy One, blessed be He Himself visits our judgment on the Day of Atonement, and He is the source of mercy. Therefore we are able to hope that also sick persons like these will enter into His hospital, which is a sanctuary in miniature, for this one day of Yom Kippur, and we will achieve a complete healing.

Therefore, my teachers and my masters, behold each one of us is obligated to consider himself an intermediate, which according to our words is an endangered sick person. Let it be known to you that now the Holy King will come to visit us, if there is in us still the breath of life and if we have the power of speech. Therefore whoever has the power of speech, upon him is to increase in words, meaning to increase in prayer and in supplications, so that the Lord will not consider him among the dead, for then He will receive him in the hospital. And whoever does not have the power to request and to pray to the Lord, at least let him make some sign that he is still alive. And what sign are we able to make? Only to weep.

Behold, just as the dead cannot weep, so it is hard for a man to weep, a man like this who is called in his life dead, because the weeping will come from feeling, and a man like this has already lost the feeling of Judaism. Therefore, it is upon us to fulfill what King David, peace be upon him, said in Tehillim 119:136: "*Rivers of water run down my eyes, because they observe not Your law.*" And this will be for a sign of the strong feeling that is in us and that we are alive. And then the Holy One, blessed be He will have mercy upon us and will receive us in His hospital on this holy day, that every moment brings us healing. Behold He is not like all the doctors who were sent in the war to the field of slaughter to see the killed and the wounded, who to gather in the hospital and who to bury. And understandably that the doctors are not exact then on the test in truth if he is still alive, and the doctor when he sees that his wounds are mortal and his moments are counted, even if the wounded one speaks with his lips in a whisper and hints to save him, it does not help him and the doctors do not pay attention to this and will write him in their book of the dead. And who speaks if even in his lips he does not have the power to speak except that the pulse is heard, then certainly the doctors will write him in their book of the dead. But the Holy One, blessed be He, as it were, is not so. Like what the poet said *'And all believe that He answers the whisper*' (from the High Holiday liturgy).

And behold it is written: "*Now Hannah, she spoke in her heart*" (I Shmuel 1:13) - and with all this her prayer ascended heavenward. And even more, *'He who opens a gate to those who knock in repentance*' (from the High Holiday liturgy).

And there is to explain in the way of moral lesson that the Holy One, blessed be He opens a gate as long as the sound of the pulse is heard just a little. Like what the poet said: *'While his soul is still within him, the Maker of his earthly form hopes for his repentance*' (from the Unetanneh Tokef liturgy), and it is sufficient for the understanding.

And there is to further explain this with a lovely parable. A parable of one who had sons from his first wife. And his second wife was a bad woman, and was to them for a bitterness of spirit, and withheld from them foods from the sons of the husband in the way of a stepmother. Once she traveled from her house to a certain place. And their father said to them: "My sons, now the time has come that you shall search in all the boxes and the cabinets that hold and conceal like all kinds of food and preserves, and you shall eat with all the desire of your soul without interruption. For the oppressor is not in her house, for she traveled afar for one day." The sons took the keys of the storehouses to open them, but they could not, and they returned to their father saying: "We cannot open." And their father answered them: "My beloved sons, know that the locks of my house are not like those of other people, that their locks need openings (keys) to open them. But the locks of my house are in another manner, only one needs to press strongly the heart and the tongue that are in the middle of the lock (*die hertzel un das tzingel fun shlesel*), and then immediately the storehouses will open to you without any trouble to take from them every good and pleasant portion and all kinds of preserves from all that your soul will desire. And you shall eat to the satisfaction of your soul, and you shall delight in the fatness of your soul in goodness and satisfaction without any toil and weariness."

So it is also the moral.

Did not our Sages of blessed memory say (in Tractate Yoma 20a): "*The Satan in Gematria is 364. 364 days he has permission to accuse, and on the Day of Atonement he has no permission to accuse.*"

Therefore now is the time that you are able to open all the storehouses of the spiritual abundance, the mercies and the good things, and to receive and to take from them sufficient for them (*das heist tzu kvetchen dem tzingel mit dem hartz* - that means to press the tongue with the heart), only to His service, may His name be blessed. And then easily all the storehouses of the good abundances in body and soul will open before us, and we will merit the great healings, and to the healings of the body and to the healings of the soul, and to be written and to be sealed for good and long life, and for a year of redemption and salvation, speedily in our days, Amen, Selah.
