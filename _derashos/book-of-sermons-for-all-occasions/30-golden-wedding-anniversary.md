Behold the embodiment of a half century of real bliss! Children, grand-children, great-grandchildren, relatives, friends what an inspiring sight, what an example for all newly-weds!

The celebrants are having thoughts of their own. Gone is youth; the years-fifty of them-flew by as if they were but a shadow of a bird in his flight.

Yet, what a treasure of golden memories, what a display of jeweled strings, stretched across a life-time of events! How wonderful to behold, and a thousand times more wonderful to be the owners thereof!

What is life, if not a goal, what is its accomplishment if not a purpose to be carried to a goal?

That goal was reached by the celebrants.

Fifty years ago they started out in life; un certain were their steps; difficult was the path; it was narrow and dangerous. But step by step they succeeded where so many have failed in the attempt. They were ever helping one another, ever ready to make the exacted sacrifices which life takes from all, ever ready to forget unpleasantness and to look forward to increased happiness; doing the hardest work with a smile, suffering disappointments with a grin, and always thinking of what dangers there were ahead, of the greater hardships still to overcome.

They never stumbled, for they were ever together, and two will not fall; even when one slips a bit the other's support stabilizes him.

You noticed the gender I used. She never slipped, never needed stabilization. The man is ever the real weak one of the two. He must be, if he wants to celebrate a golden wedding anniversary. You can not conquer by mere force; you can not endure by sheer physical strength.

It is good to be the recipients of God's bounty in worldly goods; but it is far more better to be under the direct Providence of God. Rare are this kind of occasions; still rarer are such celebrants as we are prvileged to entertain to-night. They are God's own protected children. In His sight they are very, very young, indeed. According to the heavenly way of reckoning of time, they are married not a half a century, but barely one hour and a quarter.

Permit me to give you the facts, and prove to you that they are not married longer than that in the records of heaven. We read in Psalm (90.4):

For a thousand years in Thy sight

Are but as yesterday when it is past.

"Yesterday," when it past out of our lives, was a space of time 24 hours long. In the eyes of God, therefore, every thousand years is but a twenty-four hour period. Thus fifty of our years, is hardly an hour and a quarter of God's years.

I pray, that God grants these celebrants at least another couple of hours-the hours in His way of reckoning-of happy life together.
