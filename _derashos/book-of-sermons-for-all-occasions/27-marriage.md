(Beneath the Huppah, after the Seven

Benedictions)

**I.**

Worthy Bride and Groom:

You are now, under the laws of this State, sanctioned by the laws of Moses and Israel, by authority, vested in me as a Rabbi in Israel, man and wife. When you ascended to this altar you were two separate beings; you are now one.

You, worthy Groom, sanctify this daughter in Israel to be your wife. She is your wife now, sanctified by you as such. The act of Sanctification-Kidushin-is significant. You did sanctify her under the law of Moses and Israel. What is this law of Moses and Israel, that mention was made of it, in preference to, as you should, perhaps, have said, the law of God?

The law of Moses and Israel is the law of God; but you spoke of the manner the law of God was received by Moses and Israel.

Standing beneath Mount Sinai the people of Israel accepted the law of God. Behold how faithful this law of God was observed by the Jewish people! They have carried it across mount and dale, prairie and everglade; they have lived up to it under all conditions, at all times, in all climes, among all peoples. They remained true to it, and no other law could entice them away from it. In their eyes it is as full of life and vigor today as it was on the day they received it.

The Torah, the law of Moses and Israel, is likened unto a Bride. Thus you have significantly made reference to the law of Moses and Israel when you sanctified this daughter of Israel to be your wife. You promised, under the term employed by you, to stand by her as does your people by the law of Moses and Israel.

And, you worthy Bride, your presence here indicates your assent and your readiness to fulfil your duties as the wife of the man of your choice. Your home will be your temple, the table therein its altar; your conduct will be the incense upon the golden altar, and your charity the offerings to God. In making the home of the man of your choice godly, and keeping it pure, you will find the full measure of happiness ready for you. May Almighty grant that it be so.

The Lord bless you, and keep you;

The Lord make His face to shine upon you,

and be gracious unto you;

The Lord lift His countenance upon you, and

give you peace, Amen.
