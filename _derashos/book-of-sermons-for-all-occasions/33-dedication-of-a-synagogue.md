Brethren:-

We are here assembled to dedicate this house of worship to the God of our fathers. We call upon Him to look down with favor from His holy Habitation, upon this work of ours, and grant us life and health so that we will be able to serve Him and love Him with all our heart, and with all our soul, and with all our might.

With a devotion rarely excelled by any religious organization the membership of this congregation left nothing to chance, and nothing undone, and with the help of God this magnificent edifice is now ready to open its doors for every one who has a desire to come and pour out his heart before his Maker.

Our rising generation, our young, will receive special attention here. We have provided for them class-rooms, where they will receive daily instructions in the religion of their fathers.

In it related in the Talmud:

"Rabbi Johanon was told that the Jews in Babylon lived to a ripe old age. He was amazed at the information, saying: " Is it not written: 'That 1 your days may be prolonged, and the days of your children upon the land which the Lord swore unto your fathers to give them'? How, then, can they become old in a strange land, not in Eretz Yisrael? And it was pointed out to him that the Babylonian Jews came to the synagogue morning and evening. Whereupon he said: 'This helps them."

How does the synagogue fill the place of "the land which the Lord swore unto your fathers to give them"?

An American ambassador, living in England, but having an American flag floating from the Embassy, actually lives on American territory, and is recognized as American territory by England.

The sacredness of Eretz Yisrael is brought into the synagogue. Here is the extra-territorial principle applied to the people of Israel. By all accepted doctrines of Judaism the Babylonian Jews were not included in the blessings of longivity, for they did not live in Eretz Yisrael, where the blessings are applicable. That was the reason of Rabbi Johanon's amazement. But when he was told that they spent some of their time at the synagogues, he understood the reason of the prolongation of their lives. They enjoyed extra-territorial privileges. They, in fact, lived in Eretz Yisrael when they came to the synagogue.

This house of God, my friends, is part of Eretz Yisrael. By coming here you will stand up for the blessing of longivity.
