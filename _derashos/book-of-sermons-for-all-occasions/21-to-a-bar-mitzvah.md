**II .**

My dear Hatan ha-Bar-Mitzvah:

A celebration in Israel, even when the pleasure appears to be entirely physical, such as we see here now:-A festive gathering, dressed in the best, sitting around beautifully decorated tables, laden with the most delicious food money can buy, of which every one partook with delight-is nevertheless, considered a spiritual celebration. It is a typical "Precept-Feast"-a Seʼudath Mitzvah.

Why such great ado about your thirteenth birthday?

Of course, it goes without saying, that you would not object to have similar celebrations in honor of your birthday every year. And, yet, let me assure you, you would not have the same feel ing, you would not enjoy the atmosphere as you do to-day.

Why?

Because today is a feast entirely devoted to your "stepping-out" and "getting-in." You stepped out from childhood,and you got in to a period of your life which will give you full recognition alike with the adults of your people, in so far as religious observances are concerned. Thus it must not be considered by you as "stepping out" and "getting in" process of an every-day nature. It is entirely connected with your future spiritual wellbeing. It is the joy of your soul. We all rejoice therein.

In one more particular sense is this festive board distinguished from an ordinary banquet. The joy of your parents, next to your own, too, is of a spiritual character. They know that they still have to continue to rear and educate you, that their real work of bringing you to successful life is just now commencing. Nevertheless, they rejoice that they were able to bring you to yours and their people and proclaim to them that you are now ready to carry the spiritual responsibility of all Israel manfully. Theirs, too, is a spiritual joy.

This is the real atmosphere of the occasion; it is heavenly, it expresses the soul of your people, and characterizes the faith, fidelity and zeal of your good parents. May their happiness and yours continue to increase in volume and contents as the years roll on, Amen.
