My Friend, the Honored Guest, Ladies and Gentlemen: -

You are deserving of the rest and recreation you are now about to enjoy. Life's duties have taken you upon their wings and carried you upon the waves of time without regard to your health, your personal fortune, your private work, even your own dear and loving family.

There is a Jewish proverb abroad:

"One who is good to the world is good only to make sacrifices of his own family."

This is applicable only to one whose family is out of sympathy with his public benefactions. And, there are quite a few instances where such is the case, both, in so far as the man and in so far as the family is concerned.

This can not be said of you, or of your family. In the very thickness of the hardest work, of the most ardent duties, your dear wife was and is your main support. She ever continues to root for you and your work. Perhaps, that alone accounts for the marvelous success of all of your undertakings.

We all will miss you, although we see no danger in your absence for the institutions which you are so jealously and zealously guarding and guiding, because of the splendid number of your co-workers, and of the personnel which you gathered around you.

In all of your joys, during your absence, we want you to know that we will find a share, when you will return in good health and good spirits. We know that you will miss all these things which are so near and dear to you. But we also know that upon your return you will find a still greater joy in your work for the benefit of the many who stand in need of your ability, integrity, honesty, honorable dealing and feeling heart.

We charge the angels of peace to accompany you, even on the gulf-course, or at any other recreation of which you are fond, and which will be of benefit to your health and vigor. And we will guard here the ministering angels to be ready to greet you when you come to us.
