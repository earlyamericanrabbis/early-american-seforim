The love of home is the strongest appeal to man's instincts. The place which we call home is as dear to us as our lives. A man's home is his castle; his domain. No one may invade it without imperiling his life, because an attempt to invade one's home is but another way of expressing an attempt of taking one's life. Home and life mean the same thing. What is life without a home? Not more than a home without life. One is a grave, limited to few square feet, and the other is a grave without boundaries at all.

Man goes to war to protect his home; man is happy to work in order to be able to have a home. A home is a man's honor; without a home he is an outcast.

A home in Israel means much more. It is the very soul of every father in Israel. It is much more than walls, rooms, a floor, a ceiling and furniture. It is to him a temple, dedicated to the service of his God and his fellow man. His table in the home is more than a mere piece of good furniture, than boards and legs; it is an altar dedicated to feed the hungry, to grace the Sabbaths and the holidays, to entertain his friends, and to offer benedictions.

All that and much more, is the meaning of a home to a mother in Israel. To her every part thereof is a limb of her own being. This part she dedicates to her child, that part to her husband; here she looks at and smiles understandingly, and there her heart is filled with pride. Her entire life is merged with her home. Every particle thereof is an artery in her life's outflow of things, both, material and sentimental.

A Jewish home must have a battlement built on its roof, so that no one will be injured thereon; it must have a Mezuzah, so that every one will know that therein lives a faithful family of Israel, and so that it be a token to those who are entitled to it that this home is ever open for good tidings as well as for evil, and that none is ever to be turned away empty-handed in case of evil tidings.

Our friends who are celebrating this housewarming, or, rather, dedication, started out right. They opened the home with thanksgiving. May they live therein in happiness, and continue to receive the favors of Almighty so that their lives will consist of happy and prosperous days, weeks, months and years, Amen.
