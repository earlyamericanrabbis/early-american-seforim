Yet another mile-post in your young life is to be reached by you. You are now ready to enter college, to acquire a profession, to get ready for your station in life.

You have graduated high-school. You have also gained a liberal knowledge in the lore of your people; you have received an early training in the religion of your father and mother. You are on the right path. You will encounter some difficulties on your journey, before you will be able to stand alone, on your own feet; but ever remember that life without effort, life without hard work is a life of a drone, and that life of a hanger-on in the rear of things, at odds and ends, is the life of a drudge.

Life is a race. The better man wins. But the lazy man has no chance of even being an entrant. He simply stays out from everything, and is but a dull spectator, which brings him neither profit nor inspiration.

Your past conduct has all of the material for the prediction of your future success. Whatever you started out to do till now you succeeded in accomplishing. That is the very pivot upon which Opportunity's door hinges.

This party of friends rejoices in your success, and anticipates for you a bright future.

In parting with you, your friends realize that life does not keep together all those who spend their childhood and their school days together. There will be longing, perhaps a tear or two by some who will miss you very much; but, knowing you as we do, we have full confidence in you that regardless of the great future you will accomplish, you will ever be glad to greet us all in friendship, as I know we all will be glad to greet you!
