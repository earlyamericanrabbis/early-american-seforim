**I.**

We greet two distinguished guests on this occasion. Abraham and Elijah.

Why these two, of all our great and historic personages?

The Mohel delivers the benediction on the precept of Circumcision; the father names the precept as the Covenant of Abraham. Before either the father or the Mohel delivers the benediction, mention is made of the prophet Elijah as the "Messenger of the Covenant."

Why?

Through Abraham this covenant was given to all of his posterity. He received this token on his body with a faith no man before him nor after him could even comprehend. Abraham was the father of his people; he was the forerunner of the en lightenment of generations unborn; he stands as the symbol of Belief-Emunah.

Elijah received a reward to be present in spirit at all occasions of a Circumcision. He was of an uncompromising disposition whenever the truth was attacked. He could not tolerate falsehood, treachery, or faithlessness. He was constantly fighting evil. His life upon earth was one continuous chain of truth forged in his own hot temperament. He was Truth incarnate, the symbol Emeth.

By observing this commandment of circumcision we express our being in both, Belief and Truth-Emet ve-Emunah. Circumcision is part of our Emunah; but it is also truly scientific.

Both Abraham and Elijah belong here.

Thus the joy of the parents and guests embraces not only the happiness of greeting a newly-initiated member but also of greeting two distinguished guests, Abraham and Elijah -Emunah and Emeth.
