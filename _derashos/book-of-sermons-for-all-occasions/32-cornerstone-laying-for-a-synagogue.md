Friends:-

We are gathered here in the name of the God of Israel to consecrate this ground, and lay thereon a cornerstone for a house of worship of our community. It is not for us, not for our sake, that we are here assembled, but we are all here to glorify the Name of our wonder-working God, who preserved us and kept us alive till this season.

Our own Temple we have no more. May it be built in our day on the sacred ground selected therefor by Almighty God Himself!

We are in need of a house of worship, a place where we and our children may gather from time to time and pour out our hearts to our Father in heaven. We have homes for our bodies. Let us have a home for our souls. We have homes for our own sakes, let us have a home for the sake of God.

Our work here this day, is sacred work. The foundation is laid; the contract is let; in a minute the ground will have been consecrated and the corner-stone laid. Let us remember that offerings were bounteous at the erection of the Tabernacle in the Wilderness. The rich and the poor alike brought their donations with such enthusiasm that a proclamation was needed to inform the people that they stop offering, as there was more than enough offered.

I am hoping to see the same spirit duplicated here, so that we should not have to issue a different kind of proclamation that is to say that we should not have to stop the work for lack of funds and be forced to issue a proclamation that we have reached the limit and can go on no farther.

And, as all of this work of ours on this day is to the glory of God, I implore Him to sanction our efforts and look down with favor upon our work. May this ground remain consecrated to the service it now is called to perform-to hold a house of worship dedicated to the service of God and to the fulfillment of His commandments, Amen.
