"This month (Nisan) shall be unto you the beginning of months" (Ex. 12.2).

"And the Lord showed me four craftsmen. Then said I: 'What come these to do?' And He spoke, saying: 'These the horns which scattered Judah, so that no man did lift up his head-these then are come to frighten them, to cast down the horns of the nations, which lifted up their heads against the land of Judah to scatter it." (Zechariah, 2.3-4).

"My lips utter a song when I view the appearance of four craftsmen, even the four craftsmen of Zechariah's vision; the prophet received interpretation from Thee as to the identity of each and every one of them; we pray Thee, may we see as we have heard; grant us Thy blessing of peace in this month" (Piyut of Musaph service).

"Who are the four craftsmen? Said R. Hana b. Bizna in the name of R. Simeon the Saint: 'Messiah son of David, Messiah son of Joseph, Elijah and Cohen-Zedek." (Tractate Sukko, 52b; Yalkut Shimony, Prophets, 569).

The month of Nisan ushers in the New Year for festivals and for kings in Israel. The Passover, Pentecost and Tabernacle holidays are completely identified with the life of Israel as a nation, as a political state, and as a distinct government based upon divine laws.

From the mandatory language of the Torah, saying: "This month shall be unto you the beginning of months," the precept to reckon time accurately was derived. We remember time by the impressions we get either by study, reflection, or experience. History to the people of Israel is connected with a thousand golden threads. The prayers are reminders of events which took place. We have the admonition of, "and thou shalt see and be reminded of" as repeated in connection with a number of the commandments.

The people of Israel is "a people of the book." We have no monuments outside of the spiritual collection of books in each and every generation. The greatness of each generation was not carved out in stone, bronze, or marble; our beautiful mothers were not pictured in oil of canvas. We have no holiness of beauty, but beauty of holiness.

We greet each season of the year in our own singular way. Nisan, or Abib, is the month wherein Passover must fall. If, because of the difference between the lunar and solar years, a number of days is to be added, we never make a second Nisan, but a second Adar. Not because we believe in astrological signs, or other heavenly tokens; but because Nisan must be in the spring of the year.

Nisan typifies the spring of our history, the birth of our nation; we were "young" in the "olden times."

And who among the old can forget the days of youth, the dreams, the visions, the plans, the ambitions and the disappointments?

In the days of Nisan, which is really, in our lives, the great anniversary of our birth, we live over anew the historic past, and call back to life the heroes who made our career so great, so noble, so envious.

The prophet Zechariah saw "four craftsmen" -builders who laid the foundation of our greatness, constructed the edifice of our eternity, and periodically are called back in our vision to come and repair some damage, or teach us how to repair it ourselves.

Who are they?

\(1\) Cohen-Zedek.

Who was Cohen-Zedek?

The greatest of all commentaries, Rashi says: Shem, the founder of the first institution of learning.

A craftsman, indeed.

\(2\) Elijah.

The great zealot who was ready to sacrifice his life in order to preserve the purity of the Jewish race. He is present at every circumcision ceremony. He never suffered a natural death. He typifies Israel.

\(3\) Messiah ben Joseph.

The Kabbalistic figure who will endure all sufferings in good cheer; who will pave the way for final triumph; who will show the example of what self-sacrifice may accomplish for a nation.

\(4\) Messiah son of David.

He will bring eternal liberty and freedom; final triumph.

Therefore: " My lips utter a song when I view the appearance of four craftsmen, even the four craftsmen of Zechariah's vision; The prophet received from Thee the interpretation as to the identity of each and every one of them; we pray Thee, may we see as we have heard; grant us Thy blessing of peace in this month."
