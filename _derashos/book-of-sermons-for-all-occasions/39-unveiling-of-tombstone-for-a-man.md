Beneath this sod lie the remains of one near and dear to all of you. In token of remembrance this stone was set up. During the ages this granite will testify to the faithfulness of the surviving members of this family in Israel.

This stone is a witness that beneath it lie whatever mortal parts there were during the life time of a good and upright man among the members of the house of Israel.

But the greatest monument the deceased himself left behind him. He lived by his good name, and died in his good name.

In this city of dead, behold all around you, countless graves, all marked with stones, telling the names of so many who lived and are now dead.

Judaism disowns the idea that we must continue to die during our whole lifetime. Life is not a preparatory school for death; but a vestibule to a great palace of a better life.

We need not look upon life with the eyes of pessimists.

What we are permitted to learn during our alloted span of life is but infinitesmal alongside the rest of knowledge which remains hidden between heaven and earth.

We only can analyze the conclusion of the whole matter. It is: "Fear God, and observe his commandments."

This, the deceased accomplished during his lifetime. His present state is a sealed book to all of us; we can not even comprehend a part thereof. No eye ever beheld it, save God alone.

In carrying out this last rite toward the memory of your father, you are honoring his in death, which is the most ardent wish of every father in Israel.

A tombstone is designated in Hebrew as "A token For a Living Soul." Your father was a living soul upon earth; his soul is living now, there where all mysteries are open books, where all that is between life and death lies revealed.

May his soul be shielded beneath the wings of God's Shekhinah.

And, now, by authority vested in me as a Rabbi in Israel, I declare this ground forever sacred to the memory of (name the deceased and his father), and this tombstone unveiled, so that its legend may for all times tell all who may desire to know that his faith was inherited by his posterity, and that part of his soul is within the souls of his children, sacred to them as it was to him.

May Almighty grant that the day of the quickening of the dead be speedily brought about, so that the immortal souls rejoin the mortal remains, even as we believe in perfect faith, Amen.
