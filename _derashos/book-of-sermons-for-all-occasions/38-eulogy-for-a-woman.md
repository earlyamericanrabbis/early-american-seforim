A mother in Israel is here casting her last rays upon this sinful planet.

All that was mortal in this good and virtuous mother now lies motionless.

The arms which embraced so tenderly her children lie in deathly stillness-immovable; her beaming countenance, which cast such life-imparting smiles upon her loved ones is now a mask of death.

Alas, that a mother ever comes to such pass!

In all human speech no one can find a sweeter word than that of mother. It is sweet, indeed, when you say: Mother! and there is a loving response; but how bitter is the taste to the son and the daughter when he or she calls, Mother! and only a deathly silence ensues after the echo of that longing call dies away!

A mother's love shapes the character of her children. That love is everlasting, immortal, indestructible.

A mother's teaching creates character; such teaching never dies, it is immortal, indestructible.

A mother's tears bring hope; such tears are never forgotten, they are ever pleading, forever praying.

Will all a mother's efforts remain forever sealed to doom by the hand of death?

No!

All the efforts of a mother are preserved. A good and virtuous mother can never be spoken of as dead. Her love for her children is undying; her care for them is perpetual; her teaching, everlasting, and her tears a continuous fountain of life-giving prayers.

And, because a mother is ever happy with her children, her happiness follows her soul to the heavenly spheres.

From there she will look down in a new light upon her children; from there her love will shine with a new lustre; from there her teaching will receive a new meaning; from there, along with the weeping angels, she will pour out prayers for her children. May her soul rest in peace, Amen.:
