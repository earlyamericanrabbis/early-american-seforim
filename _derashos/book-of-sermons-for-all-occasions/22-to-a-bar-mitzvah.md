**III .**

My dear Hatan Bar-Mitzvah:

Look around at this splendid gathering; see how happy every one is; behold the smile on every one's face; note the expression on your father's and on your mother's countenances. What has happened to all of them?

Nothing particular happened just now. You were born, like so many others, protesting with a loud cry in your own fashion, some thirteen years and a few hours ago. Well, what of it; you had no say in the matter.

And-here we are celebrating, despite all we may point out to the disadvantage of the event thirteen years ago.

But something really did happen then, and is happening now. One must first fully understand the position of the people of Israel among the nations to appreciate what strength a single member adds to the total number of Jews. Amidst an environment altogether unfriendly, if not real hostile, even in this age of enlightenment, the Jewish people finds itself standing on a precipice, living on a volcano, almost without means of escaping a disaster. The only safety valve to prevent disaster is the Jewish faith.

To that faith you were born. Your parents rejoiced; alike with theirs, if that is possible, was the joy of all Israel. A man-child was born; an added source of strength to Israel's weakened position. But, who could foretell what you were going to be, what you were going to do, when you will grow up? Not even your own parents. They determined, however, to bring you up to the glory of Israel and Israel's God. A bad boy pays no attention either to parent or teacher. You were a good boy; you honored your father and your mother, and you minded your teachers.

That spiritual reservoir which till now was yours, you brought to the Synagogue and made a free-will-offering thereof to your people. Whatever strength you have now belongs to your people. You gave that to them voluntarily, under the pressure of no one save your own conscience. In this you did have a say; you had the choice to be either a good boy and glorify your parents or the reverse; you chose the right path. You did glorify your Father in heaven, your father and mother on earth, and your people throughout posterity.

May God bless you, Amen.
