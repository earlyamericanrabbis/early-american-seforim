*“Harass the Midionites and smite them. For they harass you with their wiles, wherewith they have beguiled you in the matter of Peor, and in the matter of Coxbi, the daughter of the Prince of Median, their sister, who was slain on the day of the plague for the sake of Peor.”* (Bamidbar 25:10-18)

*“The rabbis taught us: Once upon a time, Rabbi Johean ben Zakkai went riding outside of Jerusalem, followed by his disciples, and he encountered young girl, picking grains of barley from beneath the feet of cattle belonging to an Arab. As the girl saw him and his disciples, she arranged her hair and addressed him, saying: ‘ Rabbi, the obligation of my support is upon you’! ‘Those daughter are you?’ asked he. ‘Ben Gurion is the family name, daughter of Nakdimon,’ replied the girl. Now, Nakdimon had been reputed to be among the wealthiest of Jerusalem’s citizens. Hearing the family name of the girl, and seeing the fate of the survivor of that great family, Ben Zakkai wept, and exclaimed ‘How happy Israel lives when they do the bidding of God! Then no nation, or tongue can master them, or theirs. But lo, and behold! when they disregard His will, the Holy One, blessed be He! turns them over into the clutches of a last-grade nation, nay, even into the merciless power of the animals of such last-graded nation’.”* (Yalkut Shimony, Bamidbar 268; Sanhedrin 82; Midrash Tanhuma Bamidbar 25)

The branding of the different groups in Israel, even as Deborah said: *“Among the divisions of Reuben, There were great resolves of heart”* (Shoftim 5:15), created a vast amount more interest in Jewish life, both within and without the ghetto walls, than the centuries of isolation were able to produce, although party affiliations, party policies and politics were unknown. Of course, some factions branded themselves almost with an alien marking-iron, and resulted in the loss of some members of the so-called upper strata by means of assimilations. Nevertheless, the very evil germinated some health giving bacteria. The vast majority knew where the boundary-line was, and where the stretch of “no-man’s” land ended.

Grouping seems to have been an inheritance of Israel. From the earliest times the aristocratic Ephraimites were considered the social arbiters of the people during the four centuries of Jewish democracy in Palestine. Judah succeeded them, after the downfall of Saul and the ascent of David to the throne of all Israel.

But all of the people agreed upon five of the most essential principles necessary for a progressive and cultured nation, namely:

\(a\) They all agreed that they were a nation, homogenous in character, directly under the Providence of Almighty Himself.

\(b\) They all remained united against a common enemy in times of distress, war, or threatened rebellion against the Torah as interpreted by their Elders, or later, by their prophets.

\(c\) They all considered themselves the trustees of God’s commandments, upon which they knew that the entire structure of a civilized life was based.

\(d\) They all accepted the Decalogue as the pivot of their entire national life, with the additional doctrines that their land was the only one spot upon this earth where the sacred experiment of Almighty was sure of success.

\(e\) They all were devoted to the cause of peace after the conquest of the promised land, their sacred heritage, to the extent that every other war was not considered necessary to the well-being of the nation.

\(a\) The people of Israel never arrogated to itself, privileges, or positions which were not accorded to it under the direct mandate of Almighty Himself. They did not deny the equal share of this earth’s blessings and of the bliss in store for the righteous of all other nations in the World-to-Come. They refused to accept alien elements in their folds through a back door. They admitted proselytes, and extended citizenship after a Sojourner had cast his lot with them for seven consecutive years. But such sojourners, at their admission to citizenship, had to live up to the laws of the Jewish nation. That was no more restricted than in most countries today and very much more liberal than in some countries in this age. Neither was the sojourner imposed upon to observe such religious laws as all Israelites had to observe. They, the people of Israel, had to observe the 613 commandments, but not so the sojourner who became a citizen. When he, the sojourner, desired to intermarry and establish a home and family, he was obliged to accept Judaism in its entirety; but he was first offered every opportunity, and given all the time he wanted, in order to desist from his determination. Of course, there was no proselytism permitted if marriage, position, money, or any other emolument was behind the attempt. The people of Israel, thus, were a nation, small numerically, with a love and devotion to its own cult, never before, or ever after, known to any other nation. Indeed, “*Not because ye are more in number than all the nations, did the Lord desire you and make a choice of you; for ye are the fewest of all nations*” (Devarim 7:7) The people of Israel, too, dreaded the warning. “*And the Lord will scatter you among the nations, and ye will be left few in number among the nations, whither the Lord will lead you*” (Ibid. 4:27); but they ever hoped for this promise: “*In thy distress, when all these things come upon thee, in the end of days - Be-ahariq Hayomim - ; then wilt thou return to the Lord, thy God, and hearken unto His voice; for the Lord thy God is a merciful God; He will not forsake thee, nor destroy thee; and He will not forget the covenant of thy fathers which He hath sworn unto them.*” (Ibid. 30-31). Israel in Eretz Yisrael was as much a minority alongside all other nations, as he was, and is, in Diaspora among the nations. But he strives, from time immemorial, to remain steadfast to his original belief and principle - to remain a homogeneous nation forever.

\(b\) From the time of Joshua’s challenge, saying: “*Now therefore, fear the Lord, and serve Him in sincerity and in truth; and put away the gods which your fathers served beyond the River, and in Egypt; and serve ye the Lord. And if it seem evil unto you to serve the Lord, choose this day whom you will serve; whether the gods which your fathers served that were beyond the River, or the gods of the Amorites, in whose land ye dwell; but as for me and my house, we will serve the Lord. And the people answered and said: Far be it from us that we should forsake the Lord, to serve other gods; for the Lord our God... drove out from before us all the people, event the Amorites that dwelt in the land; therefore we also will serve the Lord; for he is our God.*” (Yehoshua 24:14-19 q.v.), even unto this day of Arab provocation, the sole question was, Is Israel to serve strange gods, or the Lord God who hath given them the Torah? And the Response was ever the same. Every attempt to lead the people astray was met by the people with one, and the same response: “*We also will serve the Lord, for He is our God.*”

\(c\) *“Neither do they that go by say; The blessing of the Lord be upon you; We bless you in the name of the Lord”* (Tehillim 129:8). “*They that go by*”, according to the ancient sages, refers to the many nations who morally appear for awhile upon the scene of activity of this earth and “*go by*” -disappear and are no more. (Yalkut Shimony Tehillim 129). But the people of Israel, marching on with the swiftly-moving current of Time, realize that their trusteeship will never end, at least not until *“the earth shall be full of the knowledge of the Lord, as the waters cover the sea...when his resting place shall be glorious”* (Yeshayahu 11:9-10). Thus, says King David; “*Neither do they that go by say: ‘the blessing of the Lord be upon thee’; We bless you in the name of the Lord*”. When the Temple stood upon Mount Moriah, we offered sacrifices to God, as a thanksgiving for each of the reputed seventy nations upon the earth. Now, the Arabs would deny us the right to come and pray for the welfare of every city, state, and country where God has brought us. But how many of the nations, and some of them are no more, greet us with the blessing of God, or even with a friendly word? At least other nations may find a pretence for denying us the friendly hospitality we are seeking; but the Arabs, the very people who are in unlawful possession of our sacred soil, that they should act as if the Devil were driving them to suicide, is beyond the comprehension of any student of history. True, the Arabs were in possession of the Land of our Fathers for centuries. So is the blind thief continuing to steal as long as he hears no kind of noise. So is the murderer who finds a house unoccupied and invades it and takes possession, will sustain him in the day when the heirs of his victim will demand what is theirs. The people of Israel, with patient perseverance will, as trustees of God’s commandments, win out in the end.

\(d\) There are three elements in the midst of Israel, even in this day of self-determination of peoples, who stand in the way between Israel, and his prospective triumph. The three elements, or rather, groups, might be summed up in three Hebrew words: (1) *Goyim*, (2) *Ka-Goyim*, (3) *Ba-Goyim*. (1) A group of radicals with a strong leaning to get lost, such as the Communists are now. They strive hard to become completely assimilated, or, rather, to sever all connections with the historic past of their people, and be real *Goyim*. Their method was, and is, the severest in all history. Their leaders are not only Godless, but by their cruelty, they proved themselves to be entirely without humanitarian principles.[^1] Wherever they have the backing, or tacit permission of the government, Communist leaders exceeded in their barbarism any alien enemy, barring none, in all Jewish history. (2) An old party in Israel, decried by the prophets (See II Melachim 17:11; and Yechezkiel 20:32), and still advocated in our day. Their philosophy is to ape the Gentile nations in the worship of God, and in every other particular self expression. “Life unto the Gentiles” is a most favorite maxim among many groups of Western Europe and America. The trouble with them is that the brand of Judaism they stand for, is neither Palestinian nor Babylonian; they neither follow the Torah and the Mishnah, nor the Talmud and Midrashes - Formalism, ceremonialism, and few ethical principles of the prophets which lend themselves to the elasticity of the several different groups, and which are most apt to find favor in the eyes of the Gentile population. (3) Assimilationists, who are paving the way for the extinction of Israel, as, for example, the Ten Tribes were lost among the nations of the earth. The “*Ba-Goyim*” group are mainly to be found in the larger Jewish centers in London, Paris, New York, Chicago, Philadelphia, Montreal, San Francisco, and Cleveland. They are unidentified. They never join either the Temple or Synagogue and never contribute to any fund raised solely for Jewish purposes; they are however, known through non Jewish sources, as for example, when one of their number is excluded from a club, a hotel, a fraternity, or leading financial organization. What the *Goyim* did, and do, in Russia, the *Ka-Goyim* did, and do in America, but in an entirely different method. The latter drawn only their children in an ocean of hatred without providing for them the life-saving belts Judaism affords. They claim no share in Judah, nor inheritance in Israel. But the great mass of the Jewish people continue to save themselves from any deluge, be it Bolshevism, Fascism or Nazism. The Torah and the hope to return to the Land of their Fathers sustains them - now in more than a figurative manner.

\(e\) The mission of Israel is Peace! The destruction of anything wantonly is forbidden. Much less the destruction of human life. In no land can this mission of Israel be carried out, save in Eretz Yisrael. The Arabs are there. But Eretz Yisrael as it was, with the boundaries measured by King David, is large enough for both, Arab and Israel. The nations are to blame. The Promise of England was too vague. “To establish a national homeland in Palestine” - not as America, in 1921, suggested it - “Palestine as a homeland for the Jews”, or the National Homeland. The language of the Balfour declaration is a promise to “look with favor”. But the Arabs did not own the country. Turkey did. England conquered it. The League of Nations issued the mandate to England. The Arabs claim possession is itself invalidated by the fact that most of Palestine is already in the hands of the Arabs. Now, of course the “homeland” for the Jews was to be established in Palestine - a small fraction of Palestine, too will be a promise well fulfilled. Now comes the Arab and claims all of Palestine. This fact in itself is sufficient to assure the people of Israel that the Arabs will never live in peace with Israel. No human being can live together, in one not, with a serpent. Israel’s mission is peace. So was that of Isaac, from the very beginning. Ishmael did not live together with Isaac. Hagar could not be retained in the household of Abraham. The life of the Arab is far from being at par with the life of Israel. *“Seek peace, and pursue it”* (Tehillim 34:15). *Bakesh Shalom Versephelu* - there is time to seek peace, and there is a time to chase it away. Israel does not desire to shed one drop of Arab blood; but, since peace with him is impossible, the only remedy is to listen to the Voice of God, saying: *“Let it not be grievous” in thy sight because of the lad (Ishmael), and because of they bondwomen; in all that Sarah saith unto thee, hearken unto her voice”* (Bereishis 21:12).

Sarah said: “*Cast out this bondwoman and her son; for the son of this bondwoman shall not be heir with my son, even with Isaac.*” (Ibid. 10)

Let not any one in Israel say: “We must not, we cannot, we are not permitted under the laws of the Torah, to partition Palestine.” we are not partitioning it. There is no law in Judaism to prevent the Jewish people from accepting even four ells of territory in Palestine. Abraham purchased Machpelah - a piece of land to consecrate as a sepulchre, but he did not claim that the land is his. Isaac dug wells and they were stopped; but he did not raise a quarrel, saying that the land is mine; Jacob purchased a site for this tents, and did not claim that the land is his (See Sanhedrin 111a). They took what they could, never, however, admitting that that was their full share of the land.

The solution to the Arab problem rests only in the separation and complete severance of the two races. The British diplomacy in the proposed plan, while it is not as munificent as the Balfour declaration was at first interpreted to mean, is most outstanding and will solve the most serious problem modern Zionism has encountered. No better than the Midionites of old are the Arabs today. They harass the people of Israel without interruption.

Taking into consideration that among the Halutzim are many boys and girls, children of erstwhile prosperous and cultured Jewish parents, who fell victims to Hitlerism, one at the present time may encounter the same sight as Rabbi Johanon Ben Zakkai in his day encountered.

The modern Midionites will no more prevail against Israel than the ancient Midionites did. The question of partitioning Palestine is neither a legal nor a theological question. The Jewish people, by accepting the British offer, will not violate a single law, or a single thought of any Jewish authority from the day of Moses till this day. The boundaries of Palestine were extended numerous times, and according to the Talmud, they will be extended to reach out to the far corners of the earth. “*Jerusalem will spread out to be as large as the whole of Eretz Yisrael, and Eretz Yisrael, will extend its boundaries throughout the world.*” (Pesikta D’Rav Kahanna, Sabbath and New Moon, too; Yalkut Isaiah 503.)

“*Jerusalem will spread out in all its directions so that its gates will reach Damascus.*” (Safri Devarim 1.)

“*Jerusalem of the future will be the metropolis of the world.*” (Midrash Rabba, Shir HaShirim 23:10).

In accepting what England is willing to yield, does not mean either the loss of hope or the lack of a sense of responsibility. It means a solution to the Arab problem. The rest lies in the future without the least doubt as the fulfillment of all prophecies as well as to the extension of the boundaries of Jerusalem and Eretz Yisrael.

The destruction of everything sacred to the millions of Jews in White Russia, the Ukraine, and other large centers, will be known only after the world will be rid of that scourge. The confiscation of sacred property alone, when it will be part of the history of that Hurban, will stagger the minds of future generations. The author was admitted to Russia as a Rabbi and an author by special passport issued to him by the then Ambassador Krestinsky of Berlin, now under-Commissary of Foreign Affairs. What the author saw, he related to the then acting Ambassador Armour at Paris, France. The latter, in harmony with the policy of ex-President Hoover, published the whole through the Associated Press, which, at the time, created quite a furore, and was actually the first inkling of what Bolsheviki regime means to the world’s Jewry as well as to the world’s civilization.
