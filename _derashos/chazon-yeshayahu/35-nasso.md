*“Rabby (Judah Nushi) said: Why is the chapter (5) dealing with a defiled woman followed by the chapter (6) dealing with one who takes the vow of a Nazirite? To teach them: Whosoever observes the fate of a defiled woman shall separate himself from inebriety.”* (Sotah 2a)

*“The Holy One, blessed by He! said to them (Israel): Even though I charged the priests to bless you, it is I who stand by them and bless you; it is therefore the custom that the priests spread out their hands when pronouncing the benedictions, to point out that the Holy One, blessed be He! stands by us.”* (Yalkut Shimony, Bamidbar 6:710)

*“Said Rabbi Samuel b. Nahmony in the name of Rabbi Jonathan: He who teaches his neighbor’s son is considered, according to Holy Writ, as if he were his father, for it is said: ‘Now these are the generations of Aaron and Moses.. and it is, moreover, said: And these are the names of the sons of Aaron (Bamidbar 3:1-2), which implies that Aaron begat his children, but Moses instructed them.”* (Sanhedrin 19b)

Natural laws, and all those subject to them, including the lower or lesser forms of life, know of no restraint. There is not other force which prompts them to act in their singular manner. To man alone was given the earth to till; the animal kingdom, to subdue it. Men cannot change nature, but he can improve upon it, chain it, tame it, govern it. He can plant flowers, where thorns grow; he can raise lions where sheep grazed; he can control birth by sterilization, and bring on death by either weapon or drug. He rules by his intellect.

But unlimited power begats abuse. The very mediums his intellect is able to discover and perfect for the dominion of the animal kingdom, of the forces of nature, he has been, and is, employing against the more helpless, the less intellectual among his fellow-man. The individual showed the way to the groups, the groups to the communities, the communities to the nations. Men kills animals for his stomach, and fellow-men for his vanity; the one who kills animals is more butcher, usually an uneducated individual, but the one who kills his fellow-men on the battlefield is a patriot, a hero, and a ruler.

A Roman never forgave, a Grecian never forgot, an Egyptian never thought, a Russian never reflected, an Englishman never retreated, a German never gave up, a Frenchman never stopped, a Spaniard never regretted, and thus, from generation to generation the larger groups continued to best one another, to subdue one another, all of which brought about a standardized form of worship for the professional killers of man. The pretences which led peoples to kill one another varied. Religion, a woman, a river, a sea-shore, an insult, commerce, jealousy, and plain highway-robbery.

Ganging up among the underworld is but a reflection of the grouping of nationals against a neighbor. Of course, it is easy and simple to sentence a criminal to spend a time in jail. In like manner, small nations, peaceful nations, unprepared nations, less worldlike nations, were, and are, being sentenced to exile, to an inferior grouping, to a lower standard of living, to poverty.

There is but one underlying cause of all of these evils - lack of self-restraint. The only way to keep a great city clean, is to have every front and back yard clean. A nation is composed of the individuals constituting it; if the individuals know not that all men have identical rights under the order of things as it should be, if greed sways the individual mind, if for the sake of selfish gain the number of victims to attain is not counted, if the rights of others are never an element in a conflict, the nation acts as the individual would, if he could. A defiled woman is possible because of lack of self-restraint. So is inebriety.

The priestly family was separated from all others, did not participate in the land allotment; God alone was to be their inheritance. The soul alone is endowed with that divine power which overcomes man’s evil inclinations. In order to set up an example for the individual, the family, the neighborhood, the nation, the priests were ordained to be in the service of God. By their example the people were to be governed. Succession to the priesthood alone was not sufficient. A pure one thorough knowledge of what it means to minister to the people is necessary. To gain that, education is essential. The sons of Aaron were also the spiritual sons of Moses. He instructed them. Then alone were they the worthy members of the generations of Moses and Aaron, and true successors to Aaron. Then only can they properly spread out their hands and bless the people; then only can they point that God stands by the people.
