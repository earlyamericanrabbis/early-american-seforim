*“And behold the angels of God ascending and descending on it -the ladder. (Bereishis 28:12) Here from we learn that the angels who guide a man in Eretz Yisrael do not guide him in Diaspora”* (Midrash Rabba, Bereishis 68; Yalkut Shimony, 28:119:7).

*“The substance one acquires in Diaspora is not as a rule a blessed substance; but, said God to Jacob, when thou wilt return to the land of thy fathers, I shall be with thee”* (Yalkut Shimony, 31:130).

Jacob knew Esau, and he also had an idea who his uncle Laban was. He fled from the murderous intentions of Esau, and directed his footsteps towards Mesopotamia, to the home of Laban. Night fell upon him; weariness so overcame him; upon the stones he lied down and peaceful slumber came to him. He did not dream that Esau and Laban, both, repented and became civilized human beings and recognized his right to live; neither did he dream that one or both died and left the entire field of activity free for him; he did not even dream of the reception awaiting him at his uncle’s house, nor of the disappointments that he would have to face. Instead, he saw a ladder, angels and heaven, and heard the Voice of God.

Unlike the love of other nations for their homeland is the love of Israel for his land. The most extraordinary and strange phenomenon in all history is the love of a people for a land they had not enjoyed of its bounties for more than eighteen centuries, and hardly ever before that did their ancestors enjoy peace and plenty for more than short intervals during fourteen centuries. Love for country and home is strong among all highly developed peoples, but both, country and home, are forgotten in but few generations, and new ties are made and new connections established, and thus the current of life is continued among the several races of mankind. Not so with Israel. His earthly Jerusalem built by David is destroyed; but the heavenly Jerusalem, built by Almighty Himself, follows him, or rather, precedes him where to he is exiled. His earthly Temple is desolate; but the Lord’s Temple, erected by His Own Hand is an ever-present structure of his conscious mind.

Moreover, in all of his wanderings, while bestowing of his best upon mankind, Israel did not lose his own spirituality. The more he gave to others the less diminished his own treasured ideals. The pious were hungry for the precepts possible to fulfill only in Eretz Yisrael; the progressives yearned for an enlightened self-expression of their own; the radicals created out of their own flesh and blood movements for countless millions to follow and enjoy freedom and happiness, while they themselves remained, consciously or unconsciously, firmly and deeply rooted in the soil of Israels spiritual Zion.

Movement creates refreshing vigor; stagnation breeds disease. For a people to make progress and advance in its own land for a number of centuries, it is necessary to have a constant inflow of new ideas, ideals, movements, and parties; otherwise the masses are left far behind the swiftly-moving column of progress, and stagnations, or worse, sets in the body politic and thus becomes an easy prey for the ever-present conqueror. The universe is governed by fixed, natural laws; so are the lower animals. Not so mankind. Natural laws, revealed laws, man-made laws, a press, pulpits, the stage, are still insufficient to govern mankind. More in gold is spent annually for prisons, police, courts, armies, navies, branches of government to watch, and watch out for, the criminal, the lawless, the plotter, than on all of agencies of culture, religion, the fine arts, and the education of the youth combined.

Why? — Because—

For mankind the ladder reaches heaven. Hell will not become ambitious to be heaven; or the moon to be sun; or the stars to be the oceans; or the dog to be the lion; or the mouse to be the elephant.

Jacob did not dream of Esau and Laban; but he did wonder why among men one is the pursuer and the other the pursued; why one the master and the other the slave; why one the victor and the other the vanquished; why one the a sea of pleasure and the other in an ocean of tears?

Is it all chance, accident, within the makeup of each individual, pre-destination, or all combined?

The ladder gave the answer. The angels of God were ascending and descending on it. Every act, every thought, is a messenger of Man. Man is not the animal scientists of the modern schools affirm. He could not reach even the stage they are on had he been an animal with only his cells to achieve all the does. The sages of Israel gave a name to acts of Man. *Malach*-messenger. What is the hand that strikes? but a messenger of the will in Man. The degree of angels, called Man, is the highest form of spiritual attainment even in heaven, according to Maimonides, and the Cabalists did not dispute this contention. What on the other hand, is the hand that gives alms? also a Messenger-a *Malach*.

In nature we do not find traits similar to the ones we find in the self-expression of Man’s intellect, of Man’s soul. Character-building, Character moulding, above everything else, is the most essential foundation for the stability and welfare of human society. Not education, but character, is the first necessity after bread.

The ladder was the ladder of Fate. Angels of God—*Malachey Elohim*—ascended and descended on it. It is in the power of Man to reach heaven, though he is rooted in earthliness.

The development of character, the ability to reach out toward heaven, is within the reach of every normal being. But Jacob saw only one great objective which was possible to attain—*Eretz Yisrael*. Its air imparts wisdom. Thus, the guiding angels of *Eretz Yisrael* were not the same the same guiding angels of the Diaspora. The earthly Jerusalem is not as great a protection for one’s substance as is the heavenly Jerusalem. A return to the Land of the Fathers, where every form and substance is blessed, where the heavenly Jerusalem meets the earthly Jerusalem under the blue clearness of the hills of Judaea meet, is the need, the only need of modern Israel.

Indeed, the Ladder of Jacob’s Dream was the Ladder of Fate—the fate, the destiny of his descendants—a Fate which, when it over-reaches them, when they are deserving, governs prosperity and spreads contentment!
