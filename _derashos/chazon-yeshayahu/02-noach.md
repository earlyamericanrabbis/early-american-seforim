*“God enlarge Japheth, And he shall dwell in the tents of Shem”* (Bereishis 9:27)

*“Because his name, Japheth, in Aramaic, means—enlargement, the Holy One, blessed be He! enlarged him. And, although Cyrus, a descendant of Japheth, decreed to build the Second Temple, the Shekinah rests only in the tents of Shem. Bar-Kapara says: ‘the language of Japheth shall be in the tents of Shem, which means, that the Torah shall be interpreted by means of translation, even as it is said: ‘And they read in the book, in the Law of God, distinctly, and they gave the sense, and caused them to understand the reading’ (Nehemiah, 8.8.)—the book of the Law of God, means—the Pentateuch; distinctly, means—that it was translated; and they gave the sense, means—the proper accents; and cause them to understand the reading, means—the Massoritic text’“* (Pesikta, Bereishis, 9:27; see also Talmud Yerushalmi, Megillah, chapter 4; Babylonian Talmud, Megillah, 3a; Nedarim, 37b, and the text of the Verse in Hebrew).

*“Rabbi Johanan said: ‘the deluge did not descend upon Eretz Yisrael”* (Zevachim, 113b).

*“Cursed be Canaan” (Bereishis 9:25) - Canaan was the great, great grandson of Noah; but he “blessed the Lord, God of Shem”* (Midrash Rabba, Bereishis 10; see also Sanhedrin 70b).

In relating the story of the flood, and of the events which came to pass immediately after it, the Torah depicts the characteristics of the three sons of Noah, and gives a prognostication of what was to be their respective careers when their number will spread out as three distinct races. The Torah, not being a text for the anthropological student, deals only with the moral and spiritual development of Noah’s sons. The ancient sages, in their effort to investigate and bring out the truth of every Passage in the Torah, looked for every source wherein some indication of the truth is discovered. Thus, in admiring the beauty of the Greek lanhuage, Rabbi Hyya Bar Abba gives a conclusive opinion as to the reason why the Pentateuch may be written in Greek, basing that opinion on a word play. *Yapht Elohim Le’Yephet - God & large Japheth - Ve-Yiskon Be-Oholey-Shem* - and he shall dwell in the tents of Shem, says Rabbi Hyya Bar Abba refers to the beauty we find in Japheth. “*Yapht*” is translated to mean beautifying, as *Yophy* means - beauty. He, therefore, translates the Passage as follows: “*Let the beauty of Japheth dwell in the tents of Shem*” (Megillah 9b).

But there is a great difference between the “*beauty of holiness*” and the “*holiness of beauty*”. The “*tents of Shem*” and the “*beauty of Japheth*” do not, as a rule, make a good combination for, the “*tents*” do not lend themselves to the use of a strange brush, neither do the coloring of Japheth harmonize with the atmosphere of Shem. Japhet worships beauty and Shem worships holiness. To beautify holiness does not at all mean to sanctify beauty. Nevertheless, there exists, according to Rabbi Hyya Bar Abba, a means whereby the beauty of Japheth may, in some instances, as in language, be of benefit to the tents of Shem. The sages also said: Japheth, because he is described as Shem’s *“big brother”* (Bereishis 10:21), is the greatest of the three sons of Noah (Midrash Rabba, Bereishis 10; Sanhedrin, 19b). The Talmudic sages, moreover, must have found something beside the word “*Yapht*” in order to arrive at such conclusion. If so, what is it?

“*The sons of Japheth: Gomer, and Magog, and Madai, and Javan, and Tubal, and Meshech and Tiras*” (Bereishis 10:2), *said Rabbi Samuel Bar Ami: the enumerated heads of families took up the following territories wherein they builded their national homes: Gomer, in Phrygia; Magog, in Germania* (Germany, or Germania, west of the Euphrates?); *Madai, in Hammadan* (or Macedonia? See Yoma, 10a); *Javan, in Greece* (or Macedonia? See Babor’s commentary on the Pesikta, Bereishis 10:5-6); *Tubal, in Bithinia; Meshech, in Nysia; Tiras, in Thracia*” (Midrash Rabba Bereishis ch. 37; Yalkut Shimeony, Bereishis 61; Yerushalmi, Megillah, 1.; Yoma, 10a). And, we read: “*Rabbi Simeon said: They were friendly to the sons of Israel while they were in the minority; but when they, in process of time, became the majority and gained the upper hand, they subjugated Israel. Yet, when the people of Israel, at times gained the upper hand, the sons of Japheth came with flattery, saying: ‘Are we not friends’, and invariably they got what they wanted, and were left in peace*” (Pesikta, Bereishis 10; Midrash Rabba, Bereishis 37; Rashi, I Divrei HaYamim 1:7).

Not so the heads of Shem’s descendants. They conquered no territories, established no national homes, and looked for no sovereignty. Tradition has it that Shem established a Yeshivah - the first house of learning in the annals of mankind. At a time when the descendants of Japheth had already taken possession of the most desirable lands in the Near East, Africa and Europe, the ancient Hebrews were still wandering around over Egypt, Mesopotamia and Tiberias, hanging out new roads, finding new pastures, discovering new minerals, making new implements, new weapons, and new tools, building new cities, planning new gardens and finding new marts - all of which were theirs only till others overpowered them, and took it away from them, or exiled them to take possession thereof.

The sons of Shem considered everything unholy - ugly. To deprive others of their lives in order to get their territory is ugly, unholy. They could not see the beauty of national life built upon unholy means. But the sons of Japheth saw empires and the beauty of it all. To them that was holy. The end justified the means. That kind of beauty was never to be admitted by the sons of Shem. Even to write a Mezzuza in the Greek language is forbidden. (Megillah 8b). That which is a part of the dwelling-place of the sons of Shem must not be made of, or with, the things which are unholy, although beautiful.

Japheth, from the inception till this day was and is the “big brother”, offering protection at first and expulsion, or slavery, or worse, in the end.

The sons of Shem, however, readily forgive, and always remember the better things in Japheth. They admit his superiority in the things which are superior. Parts of the civilization which the sons of Japheth did create, the sons of Shem willingly embrace and add to it. Moreover, where their power is supreme, if it be not taken away from them by force, they easily yield to the sons of Japheth, if the latter but extend a hand of tolerance, not to say friendship.

It thus becomes clear to the average observer that the real deluge, although without rain-drops, without open heavenly windows, commenced not very long after the post-deluvion era dawned. It also brings out another symble. The deluge never stopped when Noah and his three sons left the Ark. Civilizations, races, nations and cults continued to be destroyed by the flood of time. There is no anthropologist in existence to-day who can identify the sons of Japheth, or, of any of their kindred. A great deluge drowned them,

But there is one place on this earth where the flood never reached. In so far as Eretz Yisrael is concerned, Rabbi Johanan is right. That little spot, that soil which yields of its fat, or of its bounty, or of its blessings to any, save to the touch of the sons of Eber - that alone could not, and never will be drowned -not even by rivers of blood. It will command its blessings to all, as soon as the “big brother” will learn that this earth belongs to all the inhabitants thereof, and as soon as he will learn that the strong are here to protect and not to exploit the weak. Then the beauty of Japheth will be a beauty of holiness, when it will be an adornment in the tents of Shem.
