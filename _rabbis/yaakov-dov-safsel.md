Rabbi Yaakov Dov Safsel, Visker Ilui, 1888-1968. Was arrested by the Germans in WWI and freed bc of Rav Chaim Ozer, came to the US in 1921, joined the New Haven Yeshiva as Maggid shiur. moved to NY in 1930. He was mechaber of Chiddushei Divrei Yaakov.
