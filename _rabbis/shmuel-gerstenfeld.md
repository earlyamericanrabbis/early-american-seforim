Rabbi Shmuel Gerstenfeld, 1873-1958. Rav in Brooklyn and Rosh Yeshiva at RIETS. Was a prolific contributor to publications such as Pardes. Studied in Klausenberg. Received semicha from Rabbi Moshe Shmuel Glazner.
