Rabbi Yaakov Kassin, 1900-1994. Leader of the Syrian Jewish Kehilla for over 6 decades. Originally from Yerushalayim, he moved to Brooklyn to lead the community in 1932. Mechaber of several seforim that span from Shailos vTeshuvos, Halacha, Kabbalah.
