Rabbi Mordechai Aryeh Haberman, the Goligorer Rav, 1881-1945. Served as Rav in Brownsville, Brooklyn, NY. Learned in Rava, Przemsyl. Semicha from Rav Dov Berish Rappaport, Rav Nosson Habenstreit, Rav Yitzchak Shmelkes, R AZ Broyde. Mechaber of Mareh HaChodesh, and many Kisvei Yad.
