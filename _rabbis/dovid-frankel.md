Rabbi Dovid Frankel, the Husiatyner Rav, 1876-1948. Was the Av Beis Din in Husiatyner until WWII, and was stuck in America when it started. Never took a position, but was close with many gedolim at the time. Publisher of hundreds of seforim from Kisvei Yad through Dovev Sifsei Yeshanim.
