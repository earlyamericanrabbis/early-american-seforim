Rabbi Yeshayahu Glazer. 1878-1938. Served as Rav in Seattle, Ohio, Montreal, Iowa, Kansas City, NY. Mechaber of 26 seforim. Learned in Yurburg, Tavrig, Rogala, Kovna. Received semicha from Rav Yitzchak Elchanan Spector & Rav Alexander Lapidus.
