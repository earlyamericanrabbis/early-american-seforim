Rabbi Avraham Leib Benjaminson, 1884-1917. Learned in Slonim, Volozhin. Semicha given by Rabbi Yaakov Joseph. Mechaber of Emek Yehoshpat, Hamisbonain, and Milhamah be-Shalom, and Arbah Deos. He also wrote poetry, which was published by Harvard in 1994.
