Rabbi Eliyahu Mordechai Maza, 1888-1954. Rebbe in MTJ under Rav Moshe. Mechaber of a ton of seforim and kuntresim. He was the father of the comedy great, Jackie Mason (aka - Yaakov Moshe Maza) * Maza is short for מזרע אהרון הכהן, which meant he was a Kohen.
