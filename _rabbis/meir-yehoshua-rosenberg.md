Rabbi Meir Yehoshua Rosenberg, 1885-1940. Rav in Hartford, Springfield, and Holyoke, CT. Mechaber of ספר כור, המבחן על הלכות בשר וחלב, מליחה, תערובות. Was an executive member of the Union of Orthodox Rabbis. grandson of Rav Yudel Rosenberg of Montreal.
