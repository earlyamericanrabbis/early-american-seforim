Rabbi Avraham Chill, 1912-2004. Rav in Nashville; Newburgh, NY; Providence, RI; West Point Military Academy. Learned in YU & Lomza (Petach Tikvah). Received semicha from Rav Kook. Mechaber of The Mitzvot: The Commandments and Their Rationale, The Minahagim: the customs and ceremonies of Judaism, their origins and rationale, The Sidrot: Insights into the Weekly Torah Reading, and Abarbanel on Pirke Avot.
