Rabbi Shalom Pinchas (Solomon) Wohlgelernter, 1901-1976. Served as a Rav in Seattle, WA; Chicago, Detroit, NY. Worked heavily with the Vaad Hatzalah during WW2. Chose Rav Yaakov Kamenetzky to take his position in Seattle while on leave the second picture is of R Wohlgelernter (r) and Rav Yitzchak Herzog. Chief Rabbi of Israel at Föhrnwald DP to spend Shavuos with survivors in 1946.
