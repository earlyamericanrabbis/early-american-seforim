Rabbi Michoel Eliezer Forshlager, 1883-1958. Served as Rav in Baltimore. Learned in Sochatchov. Mechaber of hundreds of Kisvei Yad, never published in his lifetime. but out currently; Toras Michoel al HaTorah, al Inyanei Shas, Kedushas Michoel A close talmid of the Avnei Nezer, was asked to give shiur in Sochatchov, after declining to head the Yeshiva.
