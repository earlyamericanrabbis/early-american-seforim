Rabbi Shaul Yisroel Bolotnikov 1907-1995. Served as arrav in Sioux City, IA. Learned in Sheduva, Telshe, Slobodka, Ponovezh. Semicha given by The Ponovezher Rav. The story goes that he was asked by the Ponovezher to succeed him 3 times, and he turned his Rebbe Muvhak down 3 times.
