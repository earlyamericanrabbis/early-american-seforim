Rabbi Eliyahu Wolf Kochin, 1870-1946. Rav in Pittsburgh. He received semicha from Rabbi Shlomo haKohen (Cheishek Shlomo), Rabbi Yechiel Michel Epstein (Aruch haShulchan), and Rabbi Yitzchak Yaakov Reines. Mechaber of Sefer Aderes Eliyahu (3 volumes).
