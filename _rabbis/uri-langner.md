Rabbi Uri Langner, the Kanenitch Rebbe (1896–1970), was the mechaber of *Ohr HaMiddos*, *Ohr HaMikra*, *Ohr HaChagim*, *Pisgamei Oraisa*, *Ohr HaAggadah*, *Nechmad u'Naim*, *Ohr HaMitzvos* and *Milin d'Hespeda*.
