Rabbi Yosef Breuer, 1882-1980, Rav of KAJ in Washington Heights. Grandson of Rav S. R. Hirsch. Learned in Frankfurt Yeshiva. Mechaber of several seforim on Rav Hirsch, Minhagim of KAJ, peirushim on Navi, and Divrei Yosef al Shas.
