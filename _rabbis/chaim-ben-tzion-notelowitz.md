Rabbi Chaim Ben Tzion Notelowitz, 1887-1962. Served as Rav in Harrisburg, Louisville, and Brooklyn. Unpublished manuscripts on the Yerushalmi and Mishneh Torah, but has dozens of articles in circulation from the national Torah journals of the time.
