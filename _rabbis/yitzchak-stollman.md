Rabbi Yitzchak Stollman, 1893-1980. Rabbi in Detroit. Learned in Radin, Slobadka, Novardok. Semicha from. Rav Shimon Shkop and Rav Moshe Mordechai Epstein. One of the founders of Beth Yehuda in Detroit Mechaber of Minchas Yitzchak al haTorah.
