Rev Gershon Mendes Seixas, 1745-1816, First Native Born Jewish religious leader in the US. Served as Hazzan in Shearith Israel in NY and in Mikve Israel in Philadelphia. He was taught shechita and milah by Rabbi Joseph Jessurun Pinto.
