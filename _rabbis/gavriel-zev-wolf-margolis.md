Rabbi Gavriel Zev Wolf Margolis, 1847- 1935. Rav in Boston and NYC. Mechaber of many seforim: Shem Olam, Toras Gavriel, Chruzei Margolios, Agudas Eizov and Ginzei Margolios on Esther, Shir Hashirim, Ruth, Koheles and Eichah.
