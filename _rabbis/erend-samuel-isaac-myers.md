Reverend Samuel Isaac Myers, 1804-1878. Rav of Bnai Yeshurun in NY, breaking off into Shaarei Tefillah. He was fiercely Orthodox, fighting non-Torah centric Judaism with the likes of Rav Rice, Rav Leeser, Rav Sabato Morais.
