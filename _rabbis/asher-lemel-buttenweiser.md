Rabbi Asher Lemel Buttenweiser, 1825-1901. Was a Rebbe in Cincinatti and Philadelphia. Received semicha from Rabbi Yehuda Leib Seligman and Rabbi Shmuel Freund. Translated Sefer Tuv Taam on Shechita by Rav Aharon Tzvi Friedman.
