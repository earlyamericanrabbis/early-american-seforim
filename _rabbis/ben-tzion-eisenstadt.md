Rabbi Ben Tzion Eisenstadt, 1873-1951. Served as Rav in Brownsville. Learned in Beshenkovichi and Kletzk. Mechaber of over 40 Seforim on Jewish History, Derashos, Halacha, and Haga'os on the Noda b'Yehuda.
