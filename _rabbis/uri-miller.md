Rav Uri Miller, 1906-1972. The last Orthodox Rav of Terra Haute, Indiana. Mechaber of ESSENTIALS OF JUDAISM: FACTS OF JEWISH LIFE AN LAW FOR USE IN TRADITIONAL RELIGIOUS SCHOOLS. Served as Rav in Terra Haute, Omaha, Baltimore, and New Orleans.
