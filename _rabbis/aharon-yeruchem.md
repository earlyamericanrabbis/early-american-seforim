Rabbi Aharon Yeruchem, 1904-1969. Served as Rav in NY. Son in Law of the Sokolover Rebbr, Rabbi Dovid Halberstam. Mechaber of Sefer Ohel Rachel al Kesuvos, Gittin, & Kiddushin. One of the Rabbanim that marched on Washington in 1942.
