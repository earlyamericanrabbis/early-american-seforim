Rabbi Avraham Shlomo Borvick, 1880-1953. Served as Rav in Elmira, NY; Brockton, MA; Dorchester, MA. Founded Yeshiva Ohel Torah, Yerushalayim. Learned in Bialystok, Stutchin, Slabodka. Received Semicha from Rav Kook, the Stotchiner Rebbe, Rav Shmuel Yosef of Vilki.
