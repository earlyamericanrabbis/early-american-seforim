Rav Zvi Magence 1914-1989. Rav Magence, mechaber of ספר מגן צבי/קדושת הארץ, was a Polish born Rav that was a Rav in St. Louis for 35 years. He learned under Rav Elchanan Wasserman, Rav Shimon Shkop. and Rav Avraham Yitzchak haCohen Kook.
