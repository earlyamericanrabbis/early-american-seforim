Rabbi Chaim Hirschensohn, 1857-1935. Chief Rabbi of Hoboken, NJ. Mechaber of Eleh Divrei haBris, Malki baKodesh, Chiddushei HaRachah, Ateres Chachamim, Ateres Zaken, Yeshuos Yaakov, Yamim MKedem, Namukei Rashi, Toras HaChinuch HaYisraeli, Berurei HaMiddos, Motzei Mayim, Musagei Shave veHaEmes, Mosdos Torah SheBaal Peh.
