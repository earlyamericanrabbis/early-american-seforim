Rabbi Dr. Reuven Maier (Rabinovici) (1887–1967) served as Rav in Brooklyn, Minneapolis and Hibbing, Minnesota. He was the mechaber of *Sefer Melechet Shlomo*, *Rays of Light* and *Pillars of the World*. He was also the Rav who prepared Bob Dylan for his bar mitzvah.
