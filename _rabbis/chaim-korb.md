Rabbi Chaim Korb, 1874-1957. Rav in Harlem and Chicago, Maggid Shiur in Beis Medrash lTorah (HTC) in Chicago Mechaber of Nesivos Chaim and was given semicha by Rabbi Itzele Rabinowitz of Ponivezh and Rabbi Eliezer Gordon of Telz.
