Rabbi Dr. Nissim Ovadia, 1890-1942. Learned in Yeshiva Bikur Cholim and Beit Midrash l'Rabbanim. Was the Chief Sephardic Rabbi in Paris & fled from Nazis. Became the Sephardic Chief Rabbi of New York.
