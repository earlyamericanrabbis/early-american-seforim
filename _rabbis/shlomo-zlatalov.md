Rabbi Shlomo Zlatalov (1879–1932) served as Rav in Schenectady, New York; South Bend, Indiana; and Brownsville, New York. He learned in Lomza and Vilna and received semicha from Rav Shlomo HaKohen, the Cheishek Shlomo. He was the mechaber of *Zichron Menachem*.
