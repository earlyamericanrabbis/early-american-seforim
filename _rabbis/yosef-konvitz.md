Rav Yosef Konvitz, 1878-1944. Rav in Elizabeth. Trenton, and Newark, NJ. He also became a key member of the Agudas HaRabbanim during the tenure of Rav Eliezer Silver. Mechaber of ספר דברי יוסף. He was the son-in-law of the Ridbaz.
