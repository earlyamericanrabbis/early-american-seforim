Rabbi Shalom Elchanan Jaffe, 1858-1923. Rav in St. Louis, NY & NJ and Rosh Yeshiva at RIETS. Mechaber of Sefer Pri Eshel. Teshuva kHalacha vDivrei Emes, Simcha Shlomo, and Sho'el kInyan. He had semicha from the Netziv and Rabbi Yitzchak Elchanan Spector.
