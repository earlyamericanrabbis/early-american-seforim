Rabbi Jacques Judah Lyons, 1814-1877, Rav in Richman, VA & NY. Founded Jews Hospital (now Mount Sinai in NY). While there isn't much published, there are hundreds of letters, manuscripts, etc., on Jewish calendars, halacha, Parshiyos, in existence.
