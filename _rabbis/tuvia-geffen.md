Rabbi Tuvia Geffen, 1870-1970. Served as Rav in Canton, OH;Atlanta, GA. Learned in Kovno, Slabodka, Grodno. Received semicha from Rabbi Moshe Danishevsky, the Kovno Rav, the Ridbaz. Mechaber of Karnei Hod,Nezer Yosef. He was the Rav who made Coca Cola kosher.
