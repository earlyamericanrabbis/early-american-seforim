Rabbi Yaakov Yosef Herman, 1880-1967. While a businessman,gave shiurimnin shuls around him. Was responsible for sending many American bochurim to Europe to learn in Yeshiva, including Rav Pinchas Scheinberg, Rav Shachne Zohn, Rav Avigdor Miller.
