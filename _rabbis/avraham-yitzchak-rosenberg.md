Rabbi Avraham Yitzchak Rosenberg, 1912-1985. Served as Rav in Savannah, GA & as a Chaplain in the US Military. Helped found NCSY. son of Rabbi Yudel Rosenbergl & son in law of the Mezebizher Brownsville Rebbe, Rabbi Shmuel Avraham Rabinowitz.
