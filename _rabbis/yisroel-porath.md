Rabbi Yisroel Porath, 1886-1974. Served as Rav in Plainfield, NJ; Cleveland, OH, as well as Rosh Yeshiva in Rabbi Israel Salanter Yeshiva in NY. Learned in Etz Chaim (Yerushalayim), Ohel Moshe. Received Semicha from Rav Kook, Rav Chaim Berlin, Rav Yaakov Dovid Willowsky (Ridbaz). Mechaber of Mavo HaTalmud.
