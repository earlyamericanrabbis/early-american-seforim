Rabbi Yosef Meir Levin 1870-1926. Served as Rav in Atlanta, Cincinatti, Wilkes-Barre, PA, Jersey City, NJ. Lerned in Mir, Eiseshok, Volozhin. Semicha given by Rav Shlomo haKohen, Rav Yaakov Joseph, Rav Dr. Hillel Klein. Mechaber of Leket Yosef.
