Rabbi Alexander Rosenberg, 1903-1972. Served as Rav in Cleveland and Yonkers. The mastermind of the OU Kashrus machine from 1950-1972. Son of Rabbi Shraga Rosenberg, the Rav who cursed his kehilla in Yonkers, then switched pulpits with his son.
