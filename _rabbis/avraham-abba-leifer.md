Rabbi Avraham Abba Leifer, the second Pittsburgher Rebbe, 1918-1990. After being brought to the US by his father, he went back to Europe to learn in Rachov and Sekelheid. By 17, he was known to know Shas and Shulchan Aruch by heart.
