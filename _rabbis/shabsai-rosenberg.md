Rabbi Shabsai Rosenberg, 1851-1913. Served as Rav in Williamsburg. Learned in Liadi, Nezhin, Volkonik, Volozhin, Vilna. Received Semicha from Rabbi Yaakov Barit and Rabbi Shlomo haKohen. Mechaber of Bris Melach.
