Rabbi Shmuel Ephraim Tiktin, 1873-1970. Served as Rav in the Bronx. A prolific mechaber of articles, essays, books and seforim. His love of the Lashon HaKodesh led him to assist Ben Yehuda in reviving the Hebrew language.
