Rabbi Dr. Eliyahu (Leo) Jung, 1892-1987. Served as Rav in Cleveland, NYC. Learned in Eperies, Galante, R Hildescheimer. Received semicha from Rav Mordechai Tzvi Schwartz, Rav Kook, Rav Dovid Tzvi Hoffman. Mechaber of dozens of books, Rhythm of Life, Crumbs and Character, Israel of Tomorrow and many more.
