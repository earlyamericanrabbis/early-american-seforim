Rabbi Yaakov Yosef Hurwitz, 1870-1931. Served as Rav in Pittsburgh, PA and Lynn, MA. Learned under Rabbi Zalman Sender Kahana Shapira. Was given Semicha by Rabbi Shnuer Zalman Fradkin, Rabbi Eliyahu Kletzki. Mechaber of Chiddushei v'Kisvei HaRi'i.
