Rabbi Yosef Adler 1877-1938. Rav of Anshe Slonim, NYC. Was a Mechanech at Torah V'Dass, co-found MTJ, Was called one of the Gedolei HaDor by Rav Belsky. Passed away on the beaches of Far Rockaway in 1938, early morning, when going to tovel in the ocean.
