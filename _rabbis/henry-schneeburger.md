Rev. Henry (Pinchas Tzvi) Schneeburger, 1848-1916. Rav of several shuls in NY and Baltimore. Learned & received Semicha from R Hildescheimer. He wrote a biography of Rav Yehuda HaNasi and translated Sefer Yechezkiel for JPS in 1895.
