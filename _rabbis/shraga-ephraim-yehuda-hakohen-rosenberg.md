Rabbi Shraga Ephraim Yehuda haKohen Rosenberg, 1871-1958. Rav in Yonkers and Cleveland. Mechaber of אבני שיש, בגדי שרד, כנפי שחר, ענבים במדבר While in Yonkers, he moved to decertify a shochet. This caused a major uproar reaching back to his homeland of Hungary. The fight got so bad that the Rav went to the Bima, cursed the congregation, and switched pulpits with his son in Cleveland, Rav Alexander Rosenberg, one of the main forces behind the OU Kashrus. Generations later, his great grandson in law became a major figure in the OU - Rav Moshe Hauer, ztzl.
