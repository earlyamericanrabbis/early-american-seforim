Rabbi Yehuda Leib Ginsburg, 1888-1946, Rav in Denver. Learned under Rabbi Meir Simcha of Dvinsk, Rabbi Chaim Ozer Grodzinski, and Rabbi Aryeh Leib Rubin. Mechaber of Musar haMishna Yalkut Yehuda al haTorah, Mussar haNeviim and Keter Shabbos.
