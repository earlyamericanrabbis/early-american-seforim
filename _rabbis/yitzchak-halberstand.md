Rabbi Yitzchak Halberstand, the Bardiover Rebbe, 1880-1944. His shul was in Boro Park. He was a descendant of the Divrei Chaim and Brother in Law of the Bluzhever Rebbe. He was known as a Tzaddik and a Baal Mofes.
