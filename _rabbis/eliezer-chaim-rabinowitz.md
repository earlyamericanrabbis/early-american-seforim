Rabbi Eliezer Chaim Rabinowitz, 1845-1916. The Yampola Rebbe, a descendant faction of Skolya. The first Admor to come and settle in America. He meant to make aliyah, but was convinced to stay by his children. Set his Chassidus up in Boro Park.
