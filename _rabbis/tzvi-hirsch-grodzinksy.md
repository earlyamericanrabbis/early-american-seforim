Rabbi Tzvi Hirsch Grodzinksy, 1857-1947. Rav in Omaha, NE. Mechaber of Mikveh Yisroel, Taharas Yisroel, Mikraei Kodesh, Beis haYayin, Milei dBrachos, and Likkutei Tzvi. He was a talmid (and second cousin) to Rav Chaim Ozer Grodzinsky.
