Rabbi Shimon Schwab, 1908-1995. Served as Rav in KAJ, Washington Heights, Baltimore. Learned in Realschule, Germany, Torah Leranstalt, Telshe, Mir. Received Semicha from Rav Eliezer Yehuda Finkel, Rav Elya Boruch Kamai, Rav Chaim ozer Grodzinski. Prolific Mechaber in German, Hebrew & English.
