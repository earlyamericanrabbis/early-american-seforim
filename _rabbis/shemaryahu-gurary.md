Rabbi Shemaryahu Gurary, the Rashag, 1897-1989. Director of Tomchei Temimim network of Yeshivos, Chairman of the International Agudas Chasidei Chabad. Brother in law of the Rebbe. Learned in Tomchei Temimim. The Freidiker Rebbe asked him to say sichos at Shalosh Seudos/Reva DeRavin, a massive kibud. Was in line to take over as Nasi of the Chassidus, but was mevatel to his brother in law, The Rebbe.
