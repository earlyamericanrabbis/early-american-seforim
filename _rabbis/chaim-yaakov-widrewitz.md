Rabbi Chaim Yaakov Widrewitz, 1836-1911. Served as Rav in Brooklyn. It is written that he was given semicha by the Tzemach Tzedek. Beside for being a massive talmid chacham, he is know for the arbitration between Rav Gedaliah Halpern of Seattle and Rav Zecharia Yosef Rosenfeld of St. Louis regarding the Kashrus of the Mikvah in Seattle being filled with municipal water. He held Rav Halpern was correct. *See sefer Mei Gava.
