Rabbi Shmuel Kaufman, the Miripoler Rebbe, The Bronx, 1873-1938. Served as Rebbe in the Bronx. Mechaber of Tiferes Shmuel. Great Grandson of Reb Shmuel Kaminker, talmid of the Berditchover and the Apta Rebbe. Also Ben acher Ben of the Baal Shem Tov.
