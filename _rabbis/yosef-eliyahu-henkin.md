Rabbi Yosef Eliyahu Henkin, 1882-1973. Received semicha from the Ridbaz, Rabbi Boruch Ber Leobowitz, Rabbi Isser Zalman Meltzer and Rabbi Yechiel Michel Epstein. He was the director of Ezras Torah. Mechaber of Peirushei Eivrah, Eidus lYisroel, Lev Eivrah.
