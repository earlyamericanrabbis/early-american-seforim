Rabbi Rachmiel Kumin, 1890-1962. Served as Rav on the Lower East Side and was the Secretary for the Chofetz Chaim. Learned in Slabodka, Volzhin, Radin. Received semicha from the Chofetz Chaim. Mechaber of Karnei Ohr.
