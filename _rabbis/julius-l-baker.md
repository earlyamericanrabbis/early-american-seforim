Rabbi Julius (Yehuda Aryeh Leib) Baker (1909–1986) served as Rav in Columbus and Lancaster, Ohio. He learned in Lomza and received semicha from Rav Gershon Fiszman and Rav Avraham Mordechai Choroscsz. He was the mechaber of *Pri Yehudah*.
