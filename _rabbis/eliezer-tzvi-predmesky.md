Rabbi Eliezer Tzvi Predmesky (1881–1968) served as Rav in the Bronx. He learned in Telz, Mir and Radin, under the Chafetz Chaim, and after his marriage in the Kovno Kollel. He received semicha from Rav Yechiel Michel Epstein, Rav Zalman Sender Shapira and Rav Eliyahu Baruch Kamai. In 1943 he was among the rabbis who marched on Washington to plead for the rescue of European Jewry.
