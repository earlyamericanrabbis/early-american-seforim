Rabbi Avraham Ever Hirschowitz (1838–1925) was a Rav in Australia, Toledo and New York (twice). He received semicha from Rabbi Yitzchak Elchanan Spektor and was the mechaber of *Yohale Sarah*, on the religious duties of the Daughters of Israel, along with *Derush l'Hazkir* and *Beis Avraham*.
