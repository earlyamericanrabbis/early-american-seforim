Rabbi Tzvi Hirsch Levin, 1807-1887. Rabbi in Charleston, SC and Baltimore. Besides for being a Talmid Chacham, which is apparent in his notebooks, still extant in his family, he was an incredible leader, helping the Jewish community navigate the Civil War, including the battles in Charleston. When he passed in Baltimore, he was eulogized by the Baltimore newspapers as "The Jewish Patriarch of Maryland.".
