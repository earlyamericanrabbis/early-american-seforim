Rav Avraham Yosef Ash 1814 - 1888: Rav of Beth HaMedrash, the first Russian American congregation. Rabbi Ash established the mode of “delivery” of the divorce document through the post-office authorities, by means of duplicate marked letters sent by the representative of the husband, one to the representative of the wife and one to the residing rabbi who compares and identifies the signatures of the witnesses to the document before issuing his permit to the wife to marry another. No Kehilla in America would accept a Shochet without his approval.
